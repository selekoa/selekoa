# Yudkowsky's argument history, phase 3

Candidate pack 2 of the thread in [`../yudkowsky-arguments/`](../yudkowsky-arguments/README.md): early web captures (1998–2010), the remaining SIAI/MIRI papers and the Arbital pages created under his account, searched against pack 1's 29 takes. It is outside application data, machine-prepared and machine-audited (same model family), with no human review.

- [scope.md](scope.md): goal, relations, dating rules and search scope.
- [pack.md](pack.md): the readable pack, generated from [pack.json](pack.json).
- [audit.md](audit.md): retrieval, failures, preparation, fresh-context audit and dispositions, and the corrections after the cross-family check.
- [verification.json](verification.json): per-passage quote checks, Arbital first-revision checks and per-document quotation totals.
- [gpt-check-input-2026-10-08.md](gpt-check-input-2026-10-08.md) and [gpt-check-verdicts-2026-10-08.json](gpt-check-verdicts-2026-10-08.json): the brief for the cross-family check (blind first; the verdicts file is opened afterwards), generated by `phase3.mjs crosscheck`.
- [gpt-check-2026-10-09.md](gpt-check-2026-10-09.md) and its [JSON companion](gpt-check-2026-10-09.json): the cross-family check (run 2026-10-08); its dispositions are in the last section of [audit.md](audit.md).
- [phase3.mjs](phase3.mjs), [captures.json](captures.json), [papers.json](papers.json): retrieval and checking.

Pack 1's display rules apply: context-only passages are never shown alone, and quotation, paraphrase and generated text stay separate.
