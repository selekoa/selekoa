## Blind order and method

Independent A1 answers were saved before intentionally viewing A2; all 68 Part A answers were committed and pushed in 000cc21 before opening verdicts, audit, review note or pack. Strict input-file isolation was imperfect: the initial whole-file shell read returned some Part B excerpts before A1 and accessed the whole input. The truncated display did not show A2 quotations. This limitation is disclosed, not represented as a fully isolated experiment.

Fresh direct HTTP, sequential external source requests with at least 8 seconds per host in a temporary read-only helper. Source bodies stayed in process memory. Search results were discovery only; inspected evidence came from live primary pages/PDFs or explicit dated captures. Quotation checks allow identified PDF line-break, HTML-entity and typography normalization; they are not byte-exact claims.

120 requested items on a 210-record pack; 18 A1, 50 A2 and 52 B. No certification of the 108 records not directly sampled. Source checks, machine interpretation and human acceptance remain distinct.
