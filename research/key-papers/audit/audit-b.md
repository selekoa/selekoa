# Key-papers pack: fresh-context audit B

Same-family (Claude) fresh-context audit, 2026-10-28. Aim: refute records. No pack file was edited.

## Method

- Read `scope.md`, then `pack.json` and the sample in `audit-sample.json` (seed 20261028): `random.cites` (10), `random.authored` (8), `targeted.boundaryCounts` (12 papers, 60 cites claims), `targeted.indexVenueDates` (12 documents), `targeted.nonArxivTexts` (12 documents, one overlapping), `targeted.commonNamePersons` (10 proposed persons).
- Cites: each claim's citing text searched with `tools.mjs grep` for the identifier or title. I checked the match context: whether it is a reference to this work, a different work, a navigation or link artefact, or the citing document's own title. I also checked the page in the locator. For boundary papers I checked every cites claim, and I checked whether two citing entries are the same work.
- Versions: arXiv dates checked against the cached arXiv abstract pages (submission history). Venue facts checked against cached Crossref records, live Crossref (`api.crossref.org`), the NeurIPS proceedings page, the ICLR 2021 virtual paper list and the AEA article page. OpenReview, dblp and MIT Press refused (see retrieval failures).
- Authored: printed name searched on PDF p. 1 of the source key. Person IDs checked against `data/entities.json` and `persons.proposed`.
- Common names: the printed name and the affiliation printed in each byline, read on p. 1 (used as evidence only, not recorded). I also checked that no other pack document prints the same name without being credited.
- Two extra sweeps, prompted by findings, went beyond the sample. (a) Title-match cites where the cited title occurs inside the citing document's own title. (b) Title-match cites whose only occurrence is inside a URL. Each hit was then read by hand.

### Systemic finding: byline matching against index name forms

`authorsNotOnFirstPage` (47 documents) lists authors whose Semantic Scholar name form was not found on p. 1. Many of them are printed on p. 1 in a different form: hyphenated given names, initials or short forms. I verified these on p. 1:

- GPipe: "Yanping Huang", "Zhifeng Chen".
- GShard: "Yuanzhong Xu", "Yanping Huang".
- LaMDA: "Zhifeng Chen", "Yanping Huang", "Yuanzhong Xu", "YaGuang Li".
- Chain of Thought: "Xuezhi Wang".
- PIQA: "Jianfeng Gao".
- GPT-3: "Jeffrey Wu".
- Constitutional AI: "Jackson Kernion".

Consequences:

- Authored claims are missing for printed authors.
- `zhifeng-chen` is missing two credits.
- Persons that meet the two-paper rule are not proposed: at least Yanping Huang (GPipe, GShard, LaMDA), Yuanzhong Xu (GShard, LaMDA) and Xuezhi Wang (CoT, PaLM, self-consistency, if printed so on p. 1). Probably also Jianfeng Gao (PIQA, MathVista), Jackson Kernion (HH-RLHF, CAI), Yanqi Zhou (T5, LaMDA) and Mohammad Bavarian (Codex, GSM8K).

Proposed correction: match each index-listed author to the p. 1 byline with a normaliser that drops hyphens in given names and allows initials and short forms. Record the printed form, and re-run person proposal. Since names are recorded as printed, a hyphen-insensitive match is a matching aid only, and every merge it produces should be listed.

Aside: the LeNet text (`kp-gradient-based-learning-applied-document-1998--text`) has no usable text layer on p. 1 (font-encoding garbage). It has no authored claims, but no curation note says why.

## Summary

- random.cites: 10 checked, 9 ok, 1 false (TC260 → RMF). The follow-up found a second false RMF cite (SAIL, its own title). RMF count goes from 12 to 10.
- boundaryCounts: 12 papers and 60 cites checked. 59 cites are genuine. **GNMT falls to 4** because the Attention v5 unlinked source duplicates the Attention document; this is a rule-application issue that also inflates four other counts.
- Outside the sample: **LSTM falls from 6 to 4** (Cotra's Wikipedia link slug is false, plus the v5 duplicate). One locator should be corrected (Attention → LSTM: use p. 10).
- Versions: all arXiv dates are correct. 23 documents checked; 16 findings. Nine Semantic Scholar venue entries give the wrong year or venue: R-CNN, PIQA, DeepLab, ZeRO, SENet, BBH, WinoGrande, GShard, Nordhaus. AlexNet gives a reprint as the publication. Four wrong DOIs are kept in `externalIds` despite curation notes. Two titles are not as printed (WinoGrande, NIST AI RMF). The "arXiv:null" labels are wrong. Aghion–Jones–Jones has the wrong year. Two day precisions are finer than the publisher supports (Autor; likely LSTM).
- Recommendation: drop or replace every Semantic Scholar "venue and year" version entry not checked against the proceedings or journal page.
- authored: 8 of 8 ok.
- commonNamePersons: 10 of 10 plausibly print the same person; none needs splitting. Three very common names (Xiangyu Zhang, Jian Sun, Wei Liu) are flagged for the cross-family check.
- `zhifeng-chen` is missing 2 credits (GPipe, LaMDA). This comes from a systemic byline-matching gap (hyphenated or initialled index name forms). As a result, printed authors get no credit, and at least three persons that meet the two-paper rule are not proposed.
