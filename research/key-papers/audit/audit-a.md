# Key-papers pack: fresh-context audit A

Same-family (Claude) fresh-context audit, 2026-10-28. The auditor tried to refute the records. It did not edit the pack or any other file except this report.

## Method

- Read `research/key-papers/scope.md` and the "Interpretation" and "Earliest located texts" sections of `docs/editorial-policy.md`.
- Sample: `audit-sample.json` → `random.states` (12), `targeted.priorityClaims` (4), `targeted.shaping` (28) = 44 entries. `kp-states-delving-deep-into-rectifiers-surpassing-2015-2` is listed both in `random.states` and in `priorityClaims`, so **43 distinct records** were audited.
- For each record, `evidence[0].passage` was matched against the cached source text (`cachedText` in `tools.mjs`), page by page (split on form feeds), in two ways: a strict match that normalises only whitespace, curly quotes and line-break hyphenation, and the pack's own `quoteFound`. The page on which each passage is found was compared with the locator. Context of 500–900 characters either side was read for every passage. Paragraph and section locators were checked by reading the page text.
- For the 16 "states" records, the arXiv stamp printed in each v1 PDF was compared with the record's date, and document records in `pack.json` were read for the version and date basis.
- For the 28 "shaping" records, the citing text's reference list was searched for each "Author (year)" entry. Where the citing source's title page carries a date or revision note, it was compared with the record's `time`. Dataset entities in `data/entities.json` carry no date fields, so dates were checked against the sources' own title pages.
- Nothing was fetched online. The cached arXiv v1 PDFs carry their own version stamps, which made a separate check unnecessary.

All 41 quoted passages are verbatim on the PDF page the locator names. No passage uses an ellipsis. The two paraphrases (`hibbard`, `openai-fgf`) were read against the full source text.

## Class-level finding

**S1. The definition of "shaping" in scope.md does not match the records.** `scope.md` defines attributed-shaping records as cases "where a dataset document's own text says that the paper shaped an argument (for example 'builds on', 'motivated by', 'following')". The records, with predicate `credits_work`, actually apply a looser test: the citing text attributes a term, finding, argument or approach to the paper. That looser test is the one this audit was asked to apply, and every sampled statement passes it. Each qualification also denies influence correctly.

About half the records are plain literature-review attributions or contrasts in which the citing text does not use or build on the paper:

- `korinek-stiglitz-frey-osborne`
- `benzell-autor`
- `benzell-aghion`
- `brs-nordhaus`
- `furman-seamans-frey-osborne`
- `chinchilla-gopher`
- `reward-modeling-cirl` (in a section titled "Alternatives for agent alignment")
- `miri-agenda-cirl` ("A separate approach …", in "Related work")
- `loosemore-omohundro` and `hibbard-yudkowsky` (rebuttals)

The record kind "shaping" and the predicate name `credits_work` read as influence, even though the statements do not.

The records where the citing text does state use or derivation are:

- `reward-modeling-concrete-problems` ("We build on")
- `instructgpt-human-preferences` ("We build on")
- `nist-800-1-nist-rmf` ("Building on")
- `eo14110-nist-rmf` ("derived from")
- `openai-fgf-nist-rmf` ("informed by")
- `gans2018-bostrom` (adopts a definition)
- `korinek-stiglitz-omohundro` (uses a concept to set up a scenario)
- `clancy-besiroglu-aghion` (uses a model to illustrate)
- `eloundou-gpt4` (relies on a claim to justify its method)
- `miri-agenda-bostrom` (frames its problem against the dangers the book describes)
- `reward-modeling-debate` (uses an analogy)
- `corrigibility-*`, `yudkowsky-good`, `nordhaus-good` and `agg-intro-bostrom` (adopt a term or label)

**Proposed correction (either one):**

- Amend `scope.md` to say that `credits_work` records an express attribution of a term, argument, finding or approach to the paper, whether or not the citing text adopts it. Add a sub-field such as `attributionKind`, with values like `adopts | builds-on | summarises | contrasts | rebuts`.
- Or keep "shaping" for the "builds on / derived from / uses" set listed above, and move the summaries, contrasts and rebuttals to a separate "attributes" kind.

## Summary

- 44 sample entries, of which 43 are distinct records (one duplicate).
- **31 ok, 12 findings.** The 15 "states" records split 10 ok and 5 findings; the 28 "shaping" records split 21 ok and 7 findings.
- **Substantive (4):**
  - Two `benzell` records carry a February 2015 date for wording attested only in the October 2018 revision.
  - `yudkowsky-good` lacks the attested date of the reprint.
  - `acemoglu-restrepo` has a mislabelled date basis: SSRN "journal" instead of the NBER June 2017 revision.
- **Wording, flagging or qualification (6):**
  - The GPT-2 state-of-the-art claim is not flagged as a priority claim.
  - The GPT-3 statement drops "state-of-the-art".
  - Bahdanau: "fixed-length vector problem" is a phrase the paper does not use.
  - InstructGPT: RLHF's "original development" is over-generalised.
  - Eloundou: "both texts come from OpenAI authors" is inaccurate.
  - Agrawal et al.: a discussant's comment is called a chapter.
- **Transcription or locator (3):**
  - C-Eval: small-capitals artefact in the passage.
  - Agrawal et al.: "value- loading" line-break artefact (counted with the record's wording finding above).
  - Corrigibility (Omohundro): wrong paragraph number.
- **One class-level finding (S1):** `scope.md` defines shaping as "shaped an argument", but the records apply the looser test of express attribution. About ten of the 28 shaping records are summaries, contrasts or rebuttals that the predicate name `credits_work` overstates.
- **Incidental:** two authorship name-matching errors outside the sample.
- No priority word appears in Selekoa's voice in any sampled statement. No attribution direction is reversed, and no "Author (year)" was tied to the wrong paper.
