# Model cards compared: population under decision 1a

Prepared 2026-11-04 for the second pack. Nothing here is admitted. The tables are written from [`population.json`](population.json), which [`population.mjs`](population.mjs) generates, including the pack order.

## Rule

Decision 1a sets the collection's rows: **every card that cites the developer's frontier framework or states a determination under it, plus, for each developer, the latest card before its first framework edition.** The first pack's 20 rows stand, including four that the rule would not select (Llama 2, Phi-2, Phi-4-mini-flash-reasoning, MAI-Cyber-1.1-Flash).

Applied with the approved decisions:

- **2a (boundary).** Image, video, audio and embedding models, safety classifiers, screen-reading (GUI-grounding) models, research checkpoints and GPT-Rosalind-6 are outside the population. Image-input models with text output, Gemma, Gemini Robotics and Phi are inside it. Gemini Robotics-ER 1.5 counts in; its card is a section of the Gemini Robotics 1.5 technical report.
- **3a (dates).** A card is dated by its earliest located publication. Later revisions are versions of the same row. The tables give the date and its kind.
- **7a.** Google DeepMind's appendix cards are dated by the earliest copy located that contains them. web.archive.org refused connections from this environment, so the copies used are arXiv versions. Each is a dated copy that the developer posted. The cross-family check of 2026-11-04 retrieved the earliest Gemini 1.0 PDF capture (2023-12-06): it has no model-card appendix, so the appendix is not backdated to it.
- **14a.** Fara-7B counts only if its card cites Microsoft's framework. It does not, and neither do the three Fara 1.5 cards.
- **First framework editions**, from the dataset: RSP v1.0 (2023-09-19); FSF v1.0 (2024-05-17); Preparedness Framework beta (2023-12-18); Meta Frontier AI Framework v1.1 (2025-02-03); Microsoft Frontier Governance Framework v1 (February 2025); for xAI, the Risk Management Framework of 2025-08-20. That framework is not in the dataset (5a). xAI's earlier draft of February 2025 changes nothing, because no xAI card falls between the two.

**How "cites" was tested.** Every in-boundary card in the four inventories, plus Fara-7B, the Fara 1.5 cards, the Gemini 1.0, 1.5 and Robotics 1.5 reports and the Muse Spark report on arXiv, was retrieved on 2026-11-04 (137 documents, cache `.local/model-cards/doc/`). The text of each was searched for its developer's framework names and categories:

- Anthropic: "Responsible Scaling Policy", "RSP", "ASL-n", "AI Safety Level", "Frontier Compliance Framework".
- OpenAI: "Preparedness Framework", "Preparedness Scorecard", "Safety Advisory Group", "Frontier Governance Framework".
- Google DeepMind: "Frontier Safety Framework", "FSF", "Critical Capability Level", "CCL".
- xAI: "Risk Management Framework", "RMF", "Frontier (Artificial Intelligence|AI) Framework".
- Meta: "Frontier AI Framework", "Scaling Framework".
- Microsoft: "Frontier Governance Framework".

Each card with no hit was then searched for "framework", "threshold" and "critical capabilit", and the hits were read. A hit counts only where the card names its developer's framework or uses the framework's own categories as a finding about the model.

## Open questions on the population

- **P1. Safety reports alongside a model card (decided: a, maintainer, 2026-11-04).** Under 1a each document that cites the framework is a row. So the Gemini 3 Pro and 3.7 Flash FSF reports, the Gemini 4 Argon Safety & Security Report and the CWM model card are rows of their own, alongside the model card or report of the same model. Decision 15a's tie-break chose one document when only one per year could be taken. Under 1a there is no such choice. _Recommendation:_ one row per document, as listed. The alternative is one row per model and date, which would drop these four.
- **P2. Google DeepMind's latest card before FSF v1.0 (decided: as recommended, 2026-11-04; resolved by the cross-family retrieval, below).** The candidates are the Gemini 1.0 appendix card and four Gemma-family cards: Gemma, CodeGemma, RecurrentGemma and PaliGemma. The appendix card was first found in arXiv v2 of 2024-04-02. The Gemma-family card pages print only a revision date of 2025-02-25. Their Hugging Face repositories were created 2024-02-08 to 2024-05-12, which dates the repositories, not the cards. If PaliGemma's card was published before 2024-05-17, it is later than the appendix card. _Recommendation:_ leave the place pending; the cross-family retrieval should find the earliest Internet Archive captures of the four pages and of the Gemini 1.0 PDF; then the latest dated one takes the place, and it joins the next pack. Until then none of the five is drafted.
- **P3. Microsoft (decided: as recommended, 2026-11-04).** No Microsoft card located names the Frontier Governance Framework. That covers the Phi-4 family of 2025–2026, MAI-DS-R1, the MAI cards, Fara-7B and Fara 1.5. Phi-4 (2024-12-12) is the latest card that is clearly earlier than the framework (8 February 2025 in the first pack's reading). Phi-4-mini-instruct and Phi-4-multimodal-instruct print "Release date: February 2025", a month that cannot be ordered against 8 February. _Recommendation:_ Phi-4 stays Microsoft's pre-framework row, and no Microsoft card is added. Revisit if a February 2025 card is dated to before 8 February.
- **P4. Threshold language without the framework's name (decided: as recommended, 2026-11-04).** Grok 4.5 calls some evaluations "safety-threshold evaluations" and reports "sub-threshold" results without naming a framework. Grok 4 Fast and Grok Code Fast 1 name none either. _Recommendation:_ not in the population. A determination is "under" the framework only where the card ties it to the framework, and the rule should not infer that tie.
- **P5. Weights READMEs (decided: as recommended, 2026-11-04).** The Grok-1 (March 2024) and Grok 2 (August 2025) Hugging Face READMEs hold weights and usage instructions. They do not present themselves as model cards, so the scope's unit of record excludes them. _Recommendation:_ xAI's pre-framework card is the Grok-1 model card of November 2023. The cross-family checker has read it. It joins the pack after it is retrieved for drafting.
