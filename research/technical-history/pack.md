### 'foundation model' `th-term-foundation-model`

Usual names searched: 'foundation model', 'foundation models'.

Criterion: The phrase "foundation model(s)" names a machine-learning model trained on broad data and adapted to many downstream tasks; engineering uses (an elastic or soil foundation model) and "foundational model" are other senses.

**How we searched.** Venues: arXiv API (abstract phrase search "foundation model" and "foundation models", submitted 1991-01-01 to 2021-08-15: 24 results each); ACL Anthology bibliography with abstracts (all entries dated 2021 or earlier). Years: To 2021-08-15 (the day before the Bommasani et al. v1 submission). Methods: Phrase search in abstracts; for each machine-learning hit, the v1 (and any pre-August-2021 version) PDF was retrieved and searched for the phrase, because the arXiv API searches the latest version. Gaps: Abstracts only, except for the version checks; full texts of arXiv papers, journals and conference proceedings outside the ACL Anthology were not searched; Google Scholar not used. The ML-sense hits found (arXiv 2010.03688 AxFormer, 2102.12037 conditional VAEs, 1810.13306 AutoML survey, 2101.09858 facial affect review) carry the phrase only in versions revised in 2022 or later: their v1 texts (and 1810.13306v4, 2102.12037v2) do not contain it. No ACL Anthology title or abstract to 2021 contains the phrase. Retrieval failures: none for this search.
