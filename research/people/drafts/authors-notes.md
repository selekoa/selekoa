# Authors group: drafting notes, 2026-10-29

Part 2 of the people pack (single-record authors). `authors.json` was generated by a scratch script from `inventory.json`, the retrieval cache (`.local/people/doc/`, keys `au-by-*` for bylines and `au-id-*` for identifying pages) and the dataset's source records. Corrections go into `authors.json` directly from here on.

## Counts

- 194 single-record authors in the inventory, on 42 documents.
- **31 proposed persons**, each with one `authored` record and one identifying passage:
  - 17 on a page the person controls (homepage, CV, résumé or signed post);
  - 1 on a business-school faculty profile (Spiess);
  - 13 on the publisher's or employer's own publication page: Meta for the eleven LLaMA authors, and Google Research for Ioffe and Mané.
- **163 printed names**, each with a reason.
- All 31 bylines were matched verbatim in a fresh retrieval. `tools.mjs check` reports no problems for the claim quotes. All 31 identity quotes were matched against their cached pages by the same `quoteFound` routine.
- 19 retrieval-failure entries; some entries group several addresses.

## Findings worth noting

- **Same name, different person.** The homepage retrieved under "William Saunders" belongs to a planetary scientist and does not list the self-critique paper. It is recorded as a printed name. This is why a same-name page is not identification.
- **Three forms of one name.** The TRPO source record prints "Michael I. Jordan". The arXiv v1 byline prints "Michael Jordan". His Berkeley publication list prints "M. I. Jordan". He is held; see the open questions.
- **"Jeffrey Wu" and "Jeff Wu" are not joined.** "Jeffrey Wu" is the single-record form on the scaling-laws paper; "Jeff Wu" is credited on four other papers. No source links the two forms.
- **Co-authors on someone else's page.** Ziebarth's CV, Spiess's profile, Dafoe's research page and Everitt's and Krueger's lists name co-authors. A page identifies its owner only.
- **Publisher pages are not independent.** The Meta and Google Research pages confirm that the institution credits the name on the paper. They say nothing more about the person. The review note should decide whether they meet the scope's "institutional page" rule.
- **Contact details.** CVs and résumés carry addresses. None is quoted; passages were cut to the publication line.

## Not done in this pass

- DeepMind's publication pages (guessed slugs, HTTP 404) could identify many AlphaFold and Chinchilla co-authors at once, if the right addresses are found.
