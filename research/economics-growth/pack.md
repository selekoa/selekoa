### G01. Machine substitution for labour produces explosive growth

**Criterion:** The text states that if machines (AI systems or emulated minds) can substitute for human labour across all or nearly all tasks, so that labour can be produced like capital, the growth rate of output rises far above historical rates (by roughly an order of magnitude or more) or keeps rising. The conditions under which the text derives this are part of the record.

### G02. Automated research produces accelerating growth

**Criterion:** The text states that if machines substitute for, or improve, the producers of ideas (researchers, inventors, engineers), feedback from output to research effort makes growth accelerate: super-exponential growth, growth rates rising without bound, or a finite-time singularity. The conditions under which the text derives this are part of the record.

_Search note:_ Texts were searched for idea-production and self-improvement feedback. A model in which technology improves with production experience (Hanson 1998) is recorded as bearing on the proposition, not as articulating it.

### G04. Essential unautomated tasks bottleneck growth

**Criterion:** The text states that growth is constrained because essential tasks or inputs remain unautomated or unimproved, or are automated only slowly, so that automating many but not all tasks, or automating them gradually, does not by itself produce explosive growth (Baumol's cost disease; an elasticity of substitution below one between tasks or between information and other inputs). Reasons why automation stays incomplete or slow (technical difficulty, physical-world tasks, training data, regulation of AI training or deployment, slow adoption) belong here.

_Search note:_ G04/G05 boundary (criterion settled 2026-10-10): a constraint is recorded under G04 when it works by keeping tasks in human hands or by slowing their automation, and under G05 when it would still bind if every task were automated. A passage is classified by the mechanism its own conclusion names; other examples in the same turn or section go in its context. Moved from G05 in this pass: G05-d → G04-k, G05-e → G04-k2 (Clancy), G05-h → G04-g2 (Erdil and Besiroglu).
