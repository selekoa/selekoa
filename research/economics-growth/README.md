# The explosive-growth debate

Phase 1 of the draft [Economics of Machine Intelligence](../../docs/projects/economics-of-machine-intelligence.md) project: which documented texts state, answer or bear on generic propositions about machine intelligence and explosive economic growth, when, and under which conditions. It is a candidate research pack outside application data, machine-prepared, machine-audited and cross-checked by a different model family, with no human review.

- [scope.md](scope.md): unit of record, relations, date rule, search scope and validation checklist.
- [pack.md](pack.md): the readable pack, generated from [pack.json](pack.json).
- [audit.md](audit.md): the fresh-context audit, its findings and dispositions, and the corrections after the cross-family check.
- [gpt-check-2026-10-09.md](gpt-check-2026-10-09.md): the cross-family (GPT) check, unchanged.
- [verification.json](verification.json): per-passage quote checks against every cached version.
- [tools.mjs](tools.mjs): retrieval, search, verification and rendering.

A relation says what a passage argues about a proposition. It does not say whether the argument is right. Passages marked context-only must never be shown alone, and model-derived passages must be shown with their conditions.
