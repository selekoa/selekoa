## Every disagreement

- **G02-h: disagree — bears-on (relation retained).** The relation is defensible, but saying the later NBER version adds the Bloom estimate is wrong: the 2021 text already gives phi = -2.1. The historical inference uses a growing researcher population and roughly constant productivity growth; the 2023 expression changes wording and rounds the estimate to about -2.
- **G03-g: disagree — bears-on.** The objection targets the intelligence mechanism, while the same turn explicitly grants the history-based plausibility that G03 requires. G03 does not require past accelerations to have been caused by intelligence; that is not a necessary premise of its criterion.
- **G04-g: disagree — contests (relation retained).** The contest is supported, but its conditions omit the infinite-input approximation on automated tasks and reallocation of a fixed input stock. CES complements, fixed allocable input stock, infinite automated-task input (an approximation to very cheap, large inputs), f = 0.1, rho = -2 and automation in under ten years; finite AI input can sharply reduce the effect.
- **G05-d: disagree — bears-on.** The selected conclusion slows AGI’s takeover of tasks, not an established residual constraint after broad substitution. The turn mixes automation/diffusion limits with resources, natural time and preferences. G05’s even-after-substitution condition is not established for its cumulative conclusion.
- **G05-e: disagree — bears-on.** The accumulated bottlenecks belong to the same mixed adoption argument; the passage does not isolate G05’s post-substitution condition. Some sectors automate and become cheap; others remain limited by time, data or regulation. The cumulative result mixes G04 and G05.
- **G06-a: disagree — bears-on.** Under the literal no-sign criterion, not-near is insufficient: Nordhaus reports two positive tests despite an overall negative diagnosis. Seven tests on then-current US data and a 20% annual threshold. Narrow the criterion to no compelling near-term approach if articulation is to be kept.
- **G06-c: disagree — articulates.** Davidson explicitly accepts absent macroeconomic signs; G06 does not require the author to run an original empirical study. He limits the inference to the next couple of decades and allows fast machine-learning growth as a possible early sign.
- **G09-h: disagree — bears-on.** A 100-years-or-more extrapolation overlaps about a century and supplies no probability bound denying G09. 20% annual threshold, two positive trends, horizon from 2015; no specified sustained duration or below-10% probability.
- **G04-j: disagree — contests (relation retained).** The contest is sound, but the unqualified 25% threshold omits same-turn timing and substitution conditions; even 25% residual tasks can permit a large boost. Digital-worker expansion, automation over decades and complementarity assumptions; slowing automation to about a century or unusually difficult substitution changes the argument.
- **G06-b: disagree — bears-on.** The count includes positive signs, so it cannot articulate the literal no-sign criterion despite supporting the not-near assessment. Read with extrapolations and abstract; distinguish some positive diagnostics from a persuasive near-term approach.

G03-g and G09-h should lose their contesting earliest roles under the present criteria. G06-a’s articulating role likewise depends on clarifying the no-sign criterion; under its literal reading, Davidson’s G06-c is the earlier located qualifying statement among checked alternatives (attested 2021-07-02, versus Clancy 2023-12-23). These are recommendations, not changes or automatic promotions. G04-e and G05-n remain defensible earliest contesting holders.

## G04/G05 boundary and G09 threshold

Keep a mechanism distinction: G04 concerns incomplete/slow automation and complementary remaining tasks; G05 concerns residual resources, natural time, regulation or preferences after broad substitution. Slow diffusion can involve both. Use specific residual-constraint passages; Clancy’s selected cumulative record is mixed context. A merged constraint proposition with mechanism types is preferable to pretending this record provides a clean boundary.

Store each author’s rate definition, duration, horizon measured from the text date, and conditional/unconditional probability separately. Do not convert verbal non-exclusion into a numerical >=10% claim or treat about-century and 100-plus horizons as sharp opposition. Opposite debate positions may share a plausibility relation under this criterion; do not use it to assign forecast camps.

The definitions materially differ: Asterisk uses tenfold recent growth for at least a decade and conditional probabilities; Nordhaus uses a 20% annual threshold and century-plus extrapolation; Davidson uses more than 30% annual GWP growth by 2100; Erdil–Besiroglu v2 specifies real GWP exceeding 130% of its previous maximum. Preserve these differences rather than harmonizing them into one numerical forecast.

G06 also needs a wording decision: The criterion says no sign, whereas Nordhaus reports two positive tests and an overall not-near judgment. If the intended proposition is no persuasive evidence of a near-term approach, revise its wording and then reassess. Under the present literal wording his record bears on it.
