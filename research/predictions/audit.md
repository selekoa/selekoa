# Same-family audit (2026-11-02)

- **Auditor:** Claude, a fresh-context subagent of the preparing session (same model family as the preparer). This does not satisfy the cross-family requirement.
- **Inputs:** `scope.md`, `audit-sample.json`, `pack.json` and the cached source texts (through `tools.mjs grep` and `text`); the dataset's own passages for sources not retrievable here. The auditor was told not to open `verification.json` or the review note.
- **Sample:** 44 of 53 records ([audit-sample.json](audit-sample.json)): 19 targeted (every survey, bet and reported statement, every conditional, vague-horizon or secondary-evidence record) and 25 drawn by a seeded shuffle of the rest. In addition, the qualification of every record (all 53) was read for neutral wording.
- **Method:** each quoted passage looked up in the source, with its surrounding paragraph; then locator, event, horizon kind and wording, probability, hedges and conditions, predictor and form, source date, and the pack's own wording.
- **Findings:** [audit/findings.json](audit/findings.json). 28 records clean, 15 with minor findings, 1 material. Quotations found in 41 of 44; 3 not retrievable (the records held as awaiting retrieval). Retrieval limits: the de Garis (1989), Moravec (1999) and Yudkowsky (2000) records could be checked only against the dataset's stored passages, without their surrounding text.
