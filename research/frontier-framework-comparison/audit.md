# Audit: frontier safety frameworks compared, 2026-10-15

A bounded, fresh-context machine audit under the [editorial policy](../../docs/editorial-policy.md#machine-first-risk-based-editorial-workflow). It is not human review. The auditors are the **same model family** (Claude) as the drafting passes, so this does not satisfy the cross-family requirement; that check is prepared in [gpt-check-input-2026-10-15.md](gpt-check-input-2026-10-15.md) and has not been run.

## Method

- **Auditors.** Five fresh-context Claude subagents, run on 2026-10-15. Each received a written brief and a bounded item file: edition, facet label and criterion, and for each stated cell the quotations and locators only. They did not see mapping notes, entry notes, the drafting passes' reports, `pack.json`, `pack.md` or `scope.md`. For "not found" cells they received the claim, the recorded scope and the recorded search terms, but not the hits the drafters set aside, and were asked to falsify the claim with at least five search terms of their own.
- **Texts.** The same publisher copies retrieved on 2026-10-15 (`.local/frontier-framework-comparison/`), read through `tools.mjs grep` and `page`. No auditor reported a retrieval failure. Meta Frontier AI Framework v1.1 (not retrievable) was not in any audited set.
- **Questions per quotation:** verbatim; locator; does the passage meet the facet criterion as an operative statement of the edition (full, partial, no); would it mislead shown alone; is there a more direct passage.
- **Selection.**
  1. A random 20% of stated cells: 68 of 340 at the time of sampling, drawn with Python `random.Random(20261015).sample` over stated cells in pack order. Split into three batches by developer.
  2. Every "not found" cell at the time of sampling: 32.
  3. **Targeted sweep.** The sample found misleading cuts at a rate that the policy treats as material ("material errors expand checking to the affected source or relation family"). Every remaining stated cell in the six facets where the sample found them (threshold definition, pause, board or external oversight, both exception facets, external reporting) was then audited the same way: 91 cells, in two batches.
- Coverage: 159 of 340 stated cells (47%) and all 32 original "not found" cells. Unaudited: the other 181 stated cells, mostly in risk-domain, levels, safeguards, determination, timing, internal reporting and revision facets.

Every audited quotation was verbatim in its edition, and no audited "not found" was overturned. The main finding is different: **many quotations were cut so tightly that, shown alone, they hid a condition, a scenario or the scope of a commitment**. "Partial" and "misleads alone" were judged on the bare quotation; in the pack most such quotations already carried a note or a "partly" marker. That does not excuse the cuts, because a reader or an exported table may separate quotation from note. They were corrected as below.

## Dispositions

Corrections made (all re-verified by `tools.mjs verify`):

- **Quotations widened to carry their own condition:** GDM v3.1 disclosure to government (now includes the "If we assess that a model has reached a CCL…" trigger); OpenAI Preparedness v2 marginal-risk clause (now two quotations: the other developer's release without comparable safeguards, then the "only if" adjustment, with the conditions in a note); RSP v2.0–v2.2 pretraining pause (now includes "If the pretraining model's capabilities are comparable or greater"); RSP v2.0–v2.2 competitor footnote (now opens "It is possible at some point in the future that…"); RSP v3.1–v3.4 "Anthropic in the lead" delay (now includes "until and unless we no longer believe we have a significant lead"); RSP v3.0–v3.4 Board and LTBT approval (now includes "In the event that marginal risk analysis … plays a major role"); RSP v3.x "General upleveling" (now the operative sentence "We will make a significant effort to meet or exceed…" together with "we will not necessarily delay"); Microsoft both editions, pause (now from "If, during the implementation of this framework").
- **Better passage substituted:** OpenAI Preparedness v2 pause (the Table 1 "halt further development" cell is interleaved in the text layer; replaced by the §1 sentence on Critical safeguards during development, with the table described in a note); OpenAI Frontier Governance Framework threshold definition (the systemic-risk severity clause replaced by §2.2's description of risk tiers); GDM v3.1 level names and TCL definition (fragments replaced by complete clauses); RSP v3.1–v3.4 "strongly consider pausing" replaced by the preceding sentence ("do not preclude us from taking cautionary action, such as refraining from training or deploying models"), with the pause sentence in a note.
- **Status changed to "not found"** (new search records written by the preparer from the auditors' readings and fresh searches): OpenAI Preparedness Beta, external reporting (model access for red-teaming is not disclosure); GDM v1.0 board or external oversight and external reporting ("we will explore" and "we are exploring" are not stated roles); GDM v2.0 and v3.1 board or external oversight (internal councils and reviews only); xAI December 2025 board or external oversight (unredacted publications for red teams or agencies are not review of decisions). These five are **not** independently audited as "not found"; they are in the cross-family brief.
- **Marked "stated, partly"** with a note: GDM v1.0 safeguards; RSP v1.0 threshold definition and oversight; RSP v2.x pause; RSP v3.x board approval; Microsoft both editions threshold definition; xAI June 2026 safeguards.
- **Notes added where the scenario or scope was missing:** GDM v2.0–v3.1 competitor and discretion entries (examples of when security levels may be adjusted; governance-function determination); GDM v3.1 misalignment as part of a combined domain; OpenAI FGF Model Report exemptions (2) and (3), and the board "for oversight" wording, which is not approval; OpenAI Preparedness v2 revision route; RSP v3.x "not hard commitments" (Roadmap goals only); xAI December 2025 shutdown (incident response, optional), "benefits outweigh" (for a particular modality or release) and "Share" (listed under "may publish"); xAI December 2025 benchmark (biology and chemistry queries); Meta v2 pause ("Should either scenario arise", moderate-or-lower target); Microsoft "marginal benefits" (one of three points the deployment case must make); Microsoft 2026 holistic assessment "may also inform" (2025: "then inform").

Kept as recorded, with reasons:

- **Borderline "not found" cells.** Microsoft February 2026 and February 2025 "other domains": loss of control and advanced autonomy are recorded under the autonomy facet, whose criterion includes loss of control through such capabilities, so they are not "other". Meta v2 persuasion: the scam, phishing and grooming outcomes sit inside the cybersecurity domain and are listed among the hits set aside. Meta v2 competitor: the "most advanced models" comparison sets the framework's scope, not what Meta does depending on other developers. OpenAI FGF competitor: the "similarly safe or safer" exemption refers to the EU Code of Practice and names no other developer; whether the Code's reference models include other developers' models is outside the edition. All four are in the cross-family brief.
- **Minor locator slips** in nine entries (paragraph or sentence counts, five of them in the xAI editions; one in the OpenAI FGF threshold definition was replaced with the new passage). The PDF page is right in every case; paragraph counts were not re-derived.
- **"Partial" quotations whose cell is already "stated, partly"** or whose note already gives the missing element, where a longer quotation would breach the 400-word budget (for example the RSP v3.x Automated R&D definition, whose second condition is in the note).

## Not done

- No cross-family check yet. The blind brief covers every "not found" cell (including the five created in this audit), the pause, decision-maker and competitor cells of each framework's latest edition, and a seeded 10% sample of the other stated cells.
- No human review of any cell.
- 181 stated cells were not audited. They are in the facets where the sample found fewest problems, but a bounded sample does not certify them.
