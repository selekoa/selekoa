Machine-prepared on 2026-10-09 from a crawl of the KAK archive; not human-reviewed. Every quotation was checked by script against the downloaded text layer or, for 2023–2026, the journal.fi metadata abstract. Titles stay in Finnish; summaries are Selekoa paraphrases.

## How we searched

- **Archive crawl.** The Finnish Economic Association's archive page and its 147 issue pages (taloustieteellinenyhdistys.fi) were crawled on 2026-10-09 with `tools.mjs kak-crawl`: 2854 PDFs, all HTTP 200, reduced to their text layers. By decade: 1950s 63, 1960s 40, 1970s 40, 1980s 40, 1990s 967, 2000s 769, 2010s 629, 2020s 276, unknown issue 30. Issues 1950–1989 are whole-issue scans (1950s in parts); from 1990 one PDF per article.
- **Text layers.** 32 PDFs had no usable text layer (fewer than 500 characters) and could not be searched. Old scans have OCR errors; quotations were taken from clean stretches only.
- **Issues on journal.fi only (3/2023 to 3/2026).** journal.fi serves a bot challenge to direct requests, which was not circumvented. Its OAI-PMH interface returned 214 metadata records for 2023–2026 (titles, authors, abstracts), and these were searched instead. Full texts of these issues were not read.
- **Term search.** `tools.mjs kak-search` matched tekoäly, keinoäly, koneäly, sähköaivo, elektroniaivo, ajatteleva kone, robot*, automaatio, automatisoi*, automatisaatio, ATK, tietokone*, tietotekni*, koneoppi*, artificial intelligence, machine learning, automation and computer in every text layer (1,851 hits). Fresh-context sub-agents inspected each strong hit in context, identified the article, and classified it.
- **Classes.** _subject-AI_, _subject-automation_ and _subject-computers_: the text is about the topic. _passing_: a relevant remark in a text about something else. _method-ML_: machine learning used as an estimation method (context only). A text's class records what it is about, not whether it is economics; "economist" is recorded only where the text shows it.
- **Not covered.** Crossref has no KAK records under its ISSNs. Finnish Economic Papers, the Association's English-language journal, was not searched.

"Earliest located" below means earliest in this crawl. It is never a claim that no earlier text exists.

**Tier (maintainer decision, 2026-10-12).** Every KAK text listed here is tier 1: KAK is treated as a core economics venue for the Finnish strand, whatever the author's field. Earliest located within the crawl is therefore computed within tier 1, and the earliest-located items above are unchanged. ETLA Brief 22 is tier 1 by a separate call the same day. The class of each text is unchanged.
