# Mainstream economics and machine intelligence

A bounded Selekoa special investigation that tests the prior "Only very recently has machine intelligence begun to enter mainstream economics." It is a candidate research pack outside application data, machine-prepared and machine-audited, with no human review.

- [scope.md](scope.md): question, unit of record, tiers, search scope, retrieval rules and validation checklist.
- [pack.md](pack.md): the readable pack, generated from [pack.json](pack.json). It holds the propositions M01–M09 (M06 split into M06a and M06b), the passages by proposition, the Crossref title census, every source's tier and tier basis, leads with full references, retrieval failures and searches.
- [timeline.md](timeline.md): dated entries from 1952 to 2024, tier 1 and tier 2 separately, with full references for texts not retrieved.
- [kak.md](kak.md): Kansantaloudellinen aikakauskirja (KAK) and ETLA texts on automation, computers and AI, from a crawl of the KAK archive.
- [library-list.md](library-list.md): every book or article not retrieved, with its full reference, why it matters and what to check in it.
- [gpt-check-input-2026-10-11.md](gpt-check-input-2026-10-11.md) and [gpt-check-verdicts-2026-10-11.json](gpt-check-verdicts-2026-10-11.json): the brief for the cross-family check, and the pack's verdicts, which the reviewer opens only after a blind pass. Generated by `tools.mjs crosscheck 2026-10-11`.
- [gpt-check-2026-10-11.md](gpt-check-2026-10-11.md) and [gpt-check-2026-10-11.json](gpt-check-2026-10-11.json): the GPT check's report (run 2026-10-12). Its final disagreements were all accepted by the maintainer on 2026-10-12 and applied the same day (below).
- [audit.md](audit.md): fresh-context audits, dispositions and the passages that most need a cross-family check.
- [verification.json](verification.json): output of `tools.mjs verify`.
- [tools.mjs](tools.mjs): retrieval, census, KAK crawl, verification, rendering and the cross-family brief.

Downstream uses must keep quotations, Selekoa paraphrases (`context`) and generated text separate. They must never show a passage marked `contextOnly` on its own, and must keep each record's tier, dates and qualification visible. A relation says what a passage argues about a proposition. It does not say whether the argument is right.

## Maintainer decisions (2026-10-10 and 2026-10-11)

Applied on 2026-10-11; details in scope.md and audit.md.

- **D1. Archive captures.** Current copies are acceptable. Where an original, or the version a reader would cite, could not be retrieved, its full APA 7 reference (with DOI where one exists) is given in `pack.json` (`notRetrieved` on sources; leads), in `pack.md` and in timeline.md.
- **D2. Tiers.** A rule shared with the economics-growth pack (2026-10-11):
  - Tier 1 is scholarly work by economists in any venue, including their essays and articles in magazines.
  - "Economist" means an economics appointment, degree, address or affiliation shown in or near the text, or an established record as an economist.
  - Tier 2 is texts by non-economists, whatever the venue, and short-form commentary even by economists.
  - Core economics venues are tier 1 regardless of author.

  Leontief 1952, Frey and Osborne, and Eloundou et al. are tier 1; Einzig is tier 2. Newhouse is tier 1 by an established record as an economist (economics degrees, Harvard). Every source and lead now carries a one-line tier basis.

- **D3. Model scenarios under M05** stay as articulations. Each carries the flag "model scenario" (not the author's own claim) and stays context-only: M05-18, M05-20, M05-22, M05-24 and M05-29.
- **D4. Library list:** [library-list.md](library-list.md).
- **D5. Recent KAK volumes:** full texts for 2023–2026 stay optional; the maintainer may download them by hand.
- **D6. Cross-family check:** required before any admission. Run on 2026-10-12 and applied (see below). The packet: [gpt-check-input-2026-10-11.md](gpt-check-input-2026-10-11.md) (blind brief) and [gpt-check-verdicts-2026-10-11.json](gpt-check-verdicts-2026-10-11.json) (the pack's verdicts, to open after the blind pass). It puts earliest-located roles and timeline entries first. No model from another family was reachable from the preparing sessions, so it was run in Codex.
- **D7. Admission:** later. Nothing here goes into `data/`.

- **Context only** added on M02-17, M03-01, M05-12, M05-14, M06a-06, M07-02, M07-04, M09-08, M09-27, M09-28, M05-28, M05-42 and M09-30, each with the reviewer's reason.
