- **Tier 1:** scholarly work by economists, in any venue: journal articles, working papers, books, chapters, commission reports, and their essays or articles in magazines. "Economist" means an economics appointment, degree, address or affiliation shown in or near the text, or an established record as an economist. Anything in a core economics venue (top five, JEP, JEL, AER P&P, NBER working papers and the NBER Economics of AI volume) is tier 1 regardless of author.
- **Tier 2:** texts by non-economists, whatever the venue, and short-form commentary even by economists (blog comments, forum replies, magazine or online debates, unless a turn is itself a substantive scholarly argument).
