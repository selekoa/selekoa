## Rules the pack applies

- **Propositions** are generic and attributed to no one; each has one criterion (below). All are posed by Selekoa. The pack records what texts say, not whether the ideas are right.
- **Relations:** `articulates` (the passage meets the criterion); `contests` (it disputes the proposition or a premise it depends on; `engages-premise` if the passage, read with its section, dialogue turn or post, gives a reason against a premise, `contrary-only` if it only states the contrary); `bears-on` (relevant but missing an element of the criterion, which is named).
- **Tiers** (maintainer rule of 2026-10-11, refined 2026-10-12). Tier 1: scholarly work by economists, in any venue: journal articles, working papers, books, chapters, commission reports, and their essays or articles in magazines. "Economist" means an economics appointment, degree, address or affiliation shown in or near the text, or an established record as an economist. Anything in a core economics venue (top five, JEP, JEL, AER P&P, NBER working papers and the NBER Economics of AI volume) is tier 1 regardless of author. An author's standing as an economist is judged when the text was written; a later record does not make an earlier text tier 1 (2026-10-12). Kansantaloudellinen aikakauskirja (KAK) is treated as a core venue for Finnish texts (2026-10-12). Tier 2: texts by non-economists, whatever the venue, and short-form commentary even by economists (blog comments, forum replies, magazine or online debates, unless a turn is itself a substantive scholarly argument).
- **Earliest located:** at most one articulating and one contesting passage per proposition and tier, by the date of the checked wording (the attested date where the wording is known only from a later capture or revision), never on bears-on passages or on reported views. It is computed within tier 1; since 2026-10-12 the tier-2 earliest is a separate role (`earliest-located-tier-2`), shown beside the tier-1 role, never instead. This pack predates that role and records none. "Earliest located" means earliest within the recorded searches, never first use.
- **Model scenarios:** a result of a formal model that meets a criterion counts as articulating it but carries the flag "model scenario", because it is not the author’s own claim, and is context-only.
- **Voice:** a passage in which a text reports another author’s view carries `voice: reported` and cannot hold earliest located. In the 2008 Hanson–Yudkowsky debate (compiled by MIRI in 2013) each passage names its speaker, and each post has its own date.
- **Context-only:** a passage that misleads when shown alone (a model scenario, a reported view, a concession, a hedged or cut sentence) is flagged and never shown alone.
- **Quotations:** at most 50 words each; at most 400 words per document, about 10% for texts under 2,000 words, and 40 words for texts read only as an abstract page. "…" joins fragments of one paragraph.

## Propositions and boundaries

- **E01.** Machine intelligence could improve itself, or the design of its successors, in a way that accelerates its own capability growth. _Criterion:_ A text that states or models AI systems improving AI (their own capabilities, their successors' design, or AI research itself) so that capability growth accelerates. Accelerating output growth from automated idea production without AI improving AI is the economics-growth pack's G02 and only bears on E01.
- **E02.** Machine intelligence could come to exceed human cognitive ability in essentially all domains. _Criterion:_ A text that states or models the possibility of machine intelligence that surpasses humans across essentially all cognitive tasks (superintelligence, AGI beyond human level). Machines doing all economically valuable work more cheaply is M05 in the main pack and bears on E02 only if cognitive superiority is stated.
- **E03.** Advanced AI poses a risk of human extinction or permanent loss of control that belongs in economic analysis. _Criterion:_ A text that treats existential or catastrophic risk from AI (extinction, loss of control, misaligned goals) as a quantity or consideration in economic analysis, welfare trade-offs or policy.
- **E04.** Developers of advanced AI face incentives (racing, competition, governance structures) that matter for its safety. _Criterion:_ A text that analyses how incentives, competition or governance of AI developers affect the safety or control of advanced AI.
- **E05.** Economics has engaged with, or neglected, superintelligence, AGI and the singularity. _Criterion:_ Not a criterion for a single passage. Assessed from the dated record and from texts' explicit statements that the topic is new, neglected, speculative or now on the agenda of economics. _Note:_ The strand's counterpart of M09.

Boundaries with the other packs of the thread. Growth explosions from full automation, automated idea production without AI improving AI, long-run history, bottlenecks and wages belong to the explosive-growth pack (G01–G09): a growth passage that meets neither E01 nor E02 only bears on them. Machines doing every task more cheaply (M05 of the parent pack) bears on E02 only if cognitive superiority is stated. Non-economist origins of the ideas (Good, Vinge, Yudkowsky, Bostrom) were not searched; they appear only where an economist quotes or answers them, or as the other side of a debate an economist took part in.
