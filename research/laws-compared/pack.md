## Questions

- **Q01 Who is covered.** On whom does the instrument place the duties compared here, in its own defined terms? _Criterion:_ The defined term for the party that bears the duties (developer, provider, company, exporter), quoted from its definition or from the provision that imposes the duties. Where the instrument has tiers, each tier is named in its own defined terms.
- **Q02 Threshold.** What threshold or condition brings a model, or its developer, within those duties? _Criterion:_ A stated condition, quoted with its figure where there is one: training compute, compute cost, revenue, capability, number of users, or designation by an authority. Stated if a threshold or designation condition is quoted; partly if the instrument covers a broad class (for example, all public services of a kind) without any model-level threshold.
- **Q03 Safety framework.** Must the covered party write or adopt a document setting out how it assesses and manages risks from its models? _Criterion:_ A provision requiring the covered party to have a written framework, policy or protocol on the risks of its models. A policy on a single other subject (copyright, content labelling) does not meet it. Partly if a risk-management system or measures are required without a written document.
- **Q04 Framework published.** Must that document be published, or given to a public authority? _Criterion:_ A provision requiring the document in Q03 to be published, or submitted or made available to a public authority. Not applicable where Q03 finds no such document.
- **Q05 Risk evaluation.** Must the covered party evaluate or test its models for risks? _Criterion:_ A duty to assess, evaluate or test the model for risks, before or after it is made available. Partly if the duty is to assess something other than the model (for example, a service or a content category) or is stated only as an aim.
- **Q06 Outside assessment.** Does the instrument require, or provide for, assessment of the model or of the developer’s practices by anyone other than the developer? _Criterion:_ Stated if the instrument requires an assessment by an independent or external party or by a public authority. Partly if it only permits or empowers one (an authority “may” evaluate or request access), or provides for an assessment of filings or documents rather than of the model.
- **Q07 Incident reporting.** Must the covered party report incidents to a public authority, and within what time? _Criterion:_ A duty to report incidents, defined however the instrument defines them, to a named public authority. The deadline is quoted where stated. Partly if a reporting duty has no stated deadline, or runs to someone other than a public authority.
- **Q08 Model information.** Must the covered party publish, or give an authority, information about each model? _Criterion:_ A duty to publish a report on a model, or to draw up and supply documentation on it to an authority or to others, quoted with its trigger (for example, before deployment). A labelling or watermark duty on outputs does not meet it; it is noted where present.
- **Q09 Whistleblowers.** Does the instrument protect people who report risks or violations? _Criterion:_ A provision prohibiting retaliation against employees or others who disclose risks or violations, or requiring a channel for such disclosures. A general complaint mechanism for users is noted but does not meet the criterion. Partly if the protection applies only by reference to another law.
- **Q10 Adopted or signed.** When was the instrument adopted, signed, approved or issued, according to its own text or official record? _Criterion:_ A date with its kind, quoted from the instrument or the official legislative or gazette record. Dates of other kinds (publication, entry into force) go in Q11 and Q12.
- **Q11 In force.** When does the instrument enter into force or take effect, by its own provision? _Criterion:_ The instrument’s own provision on entry into force, taking effect or operative date, quoted. A date computed from a rule (“the twentieth day following publication”) is quoted as the rule; any computed date is marked as computed.
- **Q12 Duties apply from.** From when do the duties compared here apply, where the instrument sets a date or period different from entry into force? _Criterion:_ A provision setting a separate application date or transition period for the duties compared here, including for models already on the market. Not found if no separate date is located; the cell does not infer one.
- **Q13 Penalties.** What is the maximum penalty for breaching the duties, and on what basis is it set? _Criterion:_ A quoted maximum (amount, share of turnover, or other measure) with its basis, such as per violation or a percentage of turnover. Partly if the instrument provides for penalties without a stated maximum, or by reference to another law.
- **Q14 Enforcer.** Which public body enforces or supervises the duties? _Criterion:_ A named public body given power to enforce, supervise or penalise breaches of the duties compared here, quoted.
- **Q15 Frameworks and codes.** Does the instrument require, recognise or refer to a developer’s own safety framework, or to a code of practice or standard, and how? _Criterion:_ A provision that requires a developer’s framework, names a code of practice or standard as a means of showing compliance, deems compliance with another regime to be compliance, or otherwise refers to such documents. The relation is recorded in the instrument’s own words: requires, may rely on, deemed, refers to.

**Legend.**

- **stated**: A passage of the instrument meets the question’s criterion as worded, however conditional or narrow its scope. The condition or scope is in the note.
- **partly**: A passage has what the criterion asks for, but in a weaker form than the criterion’s own words: for example, a power (“may”) where the criterion asks for a duty (“shall”), or a duty without the deadline the question asks about.
- **not-found**: No passage meeting the criterion was found. The cell lists the sections read, the search terms and every passage set aside, with the reason. It does not show that the instrument lacks such a provision elsewhere, or that no other law imposes one.
- **not-applicable**: The question does not arise for this instrument, for the reason given (for example, there is no framework duty whose publication could be asked about).
- **not-retrieved**: The instrument’s text could not be retrieved, so the question could not be checked.
- **held**: The cross-family checker and the preparer disagree on the status. The cell shows the preparer’s status and is held, with both reasons, for the maintainer’s decision.

**Rules for partly and deadlines.** Decision 5a, approved by the maintainer on 2026-11-03 (review note, “Maintainer decisions”).

### United Kingdom: uk-no-binding-instrument

Within the searches stated below, no UK law or other binding instrument placing duties on developers of frontier or general-purpose AI models was located; this is not a finding that no such instrument exists. The 2024 King's Speech said the government would seek such legislation; the 2026 briefing notes contain none of the searched terms; the memoranda with developers state that they are not legally binding; and title searches of legislation.gov.uk for 2023–2026 found one instrument with "artificial intelligence" in its title, which places a duty on the Information Commissioner, not on developers.
