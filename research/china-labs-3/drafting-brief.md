# Drafting brief: Chinese frontier-AI institutions, third collection

The specification given to each fresh-context drafting pass. Kept as the method record; the merged pack is `pack.json`. Read [scope.md](scope.md) first. Adapted from the [second collection's brief](../china-labs-2/drafting-brief.md).

## Task

Draft candidate records for one group into `research/china-labs-3/drafts/<group>.json`. Nothing in `data/`, `src/` or the application is changed. Do not commit; the coordinator merges and commits.

Read first: `AGENTS.md` (privacy and evidence rules), `docs/editorial-policy.md` (sections "Atomic claims and sources", "Short quotation", "Interpretation"), [scope.md](scope.md). Existing records you must reuse, not duplicate: `.local/china-labs-3/existing-claims.txt` (claim ID | subject predicate object | date | statement) and `.local/china-labs-3/existing-entities.txt` (ID | type | name | description). Search them (`grep -i`) for your institutions, their people and documents before drafting.

## What to record, per developer

In this order, within the group budget the coordinator gives (about 20–30 claims per institution):

1. **Founding**: only where existing records lack it, from the organisation's own statement or a filing (`founding_stated`, literal date at the source's precision).
2. **Legal entities**: from filings (a prospectus, an annual report, an exchange announcement) and registry-derived statements (a filing's or the company's own statement of its registered name, form and registration date; the filing entity 备案单位 that a regulator's list names). `has_legal_form` (literal: the legal name and form as stated, e.g. "科大讯飞股份有限公司, a joint-stock company limited by shares") and `formed_entity` where a filing says one organisation formed another. Never infer ownership or group structure from a shared name or address. Commercial registry aggregators (Qichacha, Tianyancha, Aiqicha) are not evidence.
3. **Leaders' public roles**: `held_role` with `attributes.role` in the source's words, and `time.kind` `interval` (bounds exactly as stated, e.g. `from: "2019-06"`) or `snapshot` (the date of the document that shows the role). Only roles that a filing or the organisation's own page states; reporting only if neither exists. Never extend a snapshot into a current appointment.
4. **Model releases**: `published` from the developer to a document entity (the report, announcement, model card or repository README), at a pinned version: arXiv `vN`, a Hugging Face commit, a Git commit. The date of the cited version, with what it is (`basis: publication`); an arXiv submission date is not the model's release date. If the developer's announcement gives a different release date, say so in the time note.
5. **Licences**: the developer's statement of the licence for named weights at a pinned commit, as `published` to a licence-document entity (`documentKind: "licence"`), quoting the operative words. A custom licence is described as the text describes itself; no judgement of "open source".
6. **Funding**: `participated_in_round` (named investor in a named round), `announced_funding` (a round total as the source states it, literal) or `investment_announced`, each with the source's wording and stage. Never attribute a round's total to one investor. A filing is the closest source; company announcements next; reporting only where neither exists, marked as such.
7. **Published safety or governance statements**: frameworks, principles, commitments, safety reports, ethics committees as the organisation announces them. `published` to the document; up to about 4 `states_provision` records per document, quoted from the cited edition. What the document states, never implementation.
8. **Authorship**: `authored` only for named people already in `existing-entities.txt` or credited in a filing, where the report's author list names them unambiguously (name and affiliation). No staff directories. If identity rests on name and timing alone, leave it out and say so.

For the governance group: who published which listing when (`published`, with the CAC or another public body as subject); each listing's count and basis (`lists_participants` from the listing document, literal object, `attributes.count` and `attributes.countBasis`: cumulative or added, services or algorithms, filed or registered, as of which date, stated by the source or counted by you); for each of the seven covered developers that appear, and for the earlier collections' developers (DeepSeek, Alibaba, Zhipu, Moonshot AI, MiniMax, Baidu, ByteDance) where cheap, one `lists_participants` record per developer per listing at most for a bounded set of listings (the earliest listing in which the developer appears, and the latest listing retrieved), with `attributes.listedEntity` set to the dataset entity, the legal entity name, model or service name, filing number and filing date as listed, in the qualification. For TC260's framework editions: no new `published` records (they exist); up to 4 new `states_provision` records per edition, on provisions not yet recorded (check `existing-claims.txt`), plus `supersedes_document` only where the publisher's text says one edition replaces or updates another. Measures and standards already in the dataset are linked by listing their claim IDs in `existingUsed`, never redrafted.

## Rules

1. **One claim, one proposition**, each with evidence from a source you retrieved with the tool below and read. Never write a quote from memory or from a search snippet: copy it from `tools.mjs text`/`grep` output.
2. **Closest primary source first**: the developer's paper, repository, announcement or filing; a public body's own text. Reputable reporting (Caixin, 36Kr, LatePost, Jiemian, The Paper, Yicai, Reuters, Bloomberg, FT, SCMP and similar) only when no primary text can be retrieved: then `sourceClass: "reporting"`, evidence `sourceRole: "secondary"`, and the qualification says the record rests on reporting. **Wikipedia, Baidu Baike, Wikidata, Crunchbase, LinkedIn, Qichacha, Tianyancha and similar are never evidence** (discovery only).
3. **Chinese originals.** Chinese sources keep `language: "zh-Hans"` (or `zh-Hant`), the original title in `originalTitle` and an English `title`. Quote the Chinese original verbatim. Every evidence item whose passage or anchor contains Chinese carries `"translation": { "text": "<English rendering>", "method": "machine", "humanVerified": false }`. Never claim human translation verification. Where a term is ambiguous (开源, 承诺, 发布, 出资, 备案, 登记), say in the qualification how it is rendered and narrowed. 备案 is "filing" and 登记 is "registration"; keep them apart.
4. **Versions.** Every source carries `version`: `{ "kind": "arxiv" | "git-commit" | "hf-commit" | "filing" | "pdf" | "mutable-page", "id": "<arXiv vN / full commit hash / filing reference / file name>", "note": "<what this version is>" }`. Mutable pages are read as they stood on retrieval; say so in the time note when a date depends on them. Pin GitHub files by commit: `git ls-remote https://github.com/<org>/<repo>` gives the commit; fetch `https://raw.githubusercontent.com/<org>/<repo>/<commit>/<path>`; for history, a shallow clone in a scratch directory outside the repository (`git clone --filter=blob:none --no-checkout`) and `git log --format='%H %cs' -- <path>`. Hugging Face: `https://huggingface.co/api/models/<org>/<model>/commits/main` lists commits; fetch `https://huggingface.co/<org>/<model>/raw/<commit>/README.md`.
5. **Retrieval route: direct only.** Use `fetch <key> <url> direct`. Do not use the reader relay or any service that fetches a page on your behalf to get past a refusal, a challenge page or a block. github.com web pages, the GitHub API, the SEC and web.archive.org refuse this environment; cite no capture. When a page refuses you, times out or returns a JavaScript shell, record it in `searched` with the URL and the HTTP status, and look for another expression (a PDF, a filing, a gov.cn repost, a pinned repository file). The cross-family checker is asked to open archive copies of the pages that refused you, so list them precisely. Slow Chinese government hosts (cac.gov.cn) often reset the first connection; the tool retries.
6. **Record what a document says or that an event happened, never why.** No motives, no loaded words (poached, rival, state-backed, national champion, breakthrough, state-of-the-art, regime, propaganda), no ideological labels. Never infer state direction, influence or affiliation from funding, location, a founding sponsor, a public body's list or co-signature. "Chinese AI" is never an actor. Never write "first", "pioneered" or "coined"; where order matters write "earliest located in this pack's searches" and only with that scope. Performance and benchmark claims are out of scope.
7. **Living people: public professional roles and public authorship only.** No e-mail addresses, even inside quoted author blocks; no private life, wealth, shareholdings of individuals or compensation.
8. **Dates keep precision and kind.** `time.kind`: `event` (with `basis`: publication, announcement, revision, signature, effective, filing), `snapshot` (a state shown on a dated document), `interval` (bounds exactly as stated), `unknown`. Always a `note`. Unknown stays unknown.
9. **Announced is not realised.** A planned release, an agreed investment or an announced commitment stays at that stage (`attributes.stage`).
10. **Quotation limits.** Each quote at most 50 words (each Chinese character counts as one word); prefer 8–30. Per source: about 10% of a document under 2,000 words; otherwise 400 words or 5%, whichever is larger, at most 800. Short posts: `passageType: "paraphrase"` with a neutral English paraphrase in `passage` and a short verbatim `anchor`.
11. **Reuse.** Reuse existing entity IDs exactly (`{ "id": "stepfun", "existing": true }`). Do not duplicate an existing claim; list it in `existingUsed`. Where `data/sources.json` already holds the same document, set `datasetSourceId`.

## Retrieval

```sh
node research/china-labs-3/tools.mjs fetch <cacheKey> <url> direct
node research/china-labs-3/tools.mjs text <cacheKey> [from] [len]
node research/china-labs-3/tools.mjs grep <cacheKey> '<regex>'
node research/china-labs-3/tools.mjs words <cacheKey>
node research/china-labs-3/tools.mjs check research/china-labs-3/drafts/<group>.json
```

Prefer arXiv PDFs (`https://arxiv.org/pdf/<id>v1`) for author lists and affiliations, and abstract pages (`https://arxiv.org/abs/<id>v1`) for submission dates. Exchange filings: HKEXnews PDFs (`www1.hkexnews.hk/listedco/listconews/...pdf`) and cninfo PDFs (`static.cninfo.com.cn/finalpage/...PDF`). Run fetches sequentially; the tool paces requests per host. Cache keys: `<group>-<short-slug>`, lowercase. Check that the retrieved text is the document, not a block page or an empty JavaScript shell.

## Draft format

As in the [second collection's brief](../china-labs-2/drafting-brief.md#draft-format), with these prefixes: claims `c3-<group>-<slug>`, evidence `c3-ev-<group>-<slug>`, sources `c3-src-<slug>`; new entities plain kebab-case not clashing with `existing-entities.txt`. A draft has `group`, `searched` (one paragraph: what was searched, what refused you with URL and status, and what was left out and why), `existingUsed`, `entities`, `sources` and `claims`. Topics: `ideas` for reports and model releases, `governance` for licences, safety statements, frameworks, listings and provisions, `institutions` for legal entities, parents and units, `capital` for funding, `people` for roles and founding credits.

Run `check` until it reports no problems. Finish with a short report: claims drafted, sources, retrieval failures (URL and status), doubtful identities, omissions and reasons.
