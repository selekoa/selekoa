# Selekoa — the biography of machine intelligence

## Core idea

Selekoa is **a source-critical institutional memory and research base for machine intelligence**. “The biography of machine intelligence” remains its north star. It preserves and makes explorable the documentary history of people and institutional diaspora, ideas and intellectual lineages, institutions and organizational change, capital, governance, consequential infrastructure, and the human thought, debate and institutional movements surrounding machine intelligence.

Its immediate purpose is to accelerate rigorous human work: academic research, serious journalism, books, policy research and historical investigation. Its longer-term ambition remains a substantial historical encyclopedia of machine intelligence.

Selekoa is machine-first in research execution. Machine research is the bootstrap layer: sourced, inspectable, qualified and reusable by humans. Producing machine-generated prose or accumulating records for their own sake is not a product goal. Exploratory research must remain visibly distinct from human-curated encyclopedia material. Machine checking does not establish accuracy or human acceptance; the [editorial policy](editorial-policy.md) defines QA and curation requirements.

### One corpus, many uses

Selekoa exists to give people a head start. Machine research finds the sources, dates them, extracts the passages that matter and states each claim with its limits, so a person can begin from a checked, citable base instead of a blank page. One corpus can then serve very different work: a journal article, a dissertation chapter, a piece of journalism, a course reading list, or a public app such as an argument explorer. Each use reads a pinned dataset release and keeps its own voice; none of them changes the records. Anyone who relies on a claim in published work verifies it against the original source.

## Corpus and research interfaces

The durable asset is the evidence-backed historical corpus and its research semantics. Bounded research collections, explanatory views and exportable research starters share provenance, not automatic acceptance. The [research-corpus prototype](research-corpus.md) preserves investigated propositions and upstream findings outside application data; research records need not become graph edges.

Maps and graphs are one interface over that corpus, not Selekoa's identity or competitive objective. Dossiers, person views, document/version histories, timelines, research starters, source inspection, exports and future query interfaces are equally valid, and may be more important for a particular research task. A graph should be reduced or omitted if it does not improve historical understanding. Do not retain graph functionality simply to compete visually with adjacent network projects.

## Flagship research spines

Research spines are deep, durable programmes of documentary coverage using the same underlying evidence standards and ontology. These are current strategic directions, not finished products or claims of comprehensive coverage.

There are three spines, one per reader-facing hub (decided 2026-10-27). "Spine" and "hub" are internal words; readers see the hub name in the menu and the spine name as the hub page's subtitle.

| Hub                   | Spine (hub page subtitle)                                  |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Governance            | Frontier AI governance                                     |
| Ideas & debates       | Human thought and institutions around machine intelligence |
| Institutions & people | The institutional history of machine intelligence          |

### Frontier AI governance

Hub: Governance. This is a likely flagship. Potential coverage includes governance frameworks and revisions, charters and constitutions, boards and control structures, organizational restructurings, safety and deployment frameworks, documented commitments and policy changes, and regulatory or contractual relationships where historically explanatory.

Versioned documentary history is central: what a document said, when, what replaced it and what changed. Keep documentary revisions, announced commitments and demonstrated implementation separate.

### Human thought and institutions around machine intelligence

Hub: Ideas & debates. Here "institutions" means the forums, journals, mailing lists and organisations where the debates took place.

Cover the documented evolution of ideas, communities, institutions and disagreements: effective altruism and adjacent institutions, AI alignment and safety, accelerationist currents, and debates over catastrophic risk, governance, openness, scaling, control and technological progress. Follow the organizations, publications and people through which these ideas developed where evidence supports the relationship.

Record self-description, explicit terminology, documents, institutional participation, disagreement, citation and documented intellectual relationships. This is historical research, not ideological labelling or culture-war classification. Never assign labels such as “doomer”, “accelerationist” or “EA” from proximity or model inference. Treat contested terminology historically and source-critically.

### The institutional history of machine intelligence

Hub: Institutions & people.

Chinese frontier-AI institutional history is a priority strand inside this spine. The [bounded Chinese collection](china-atlas.md) is its beginning; on the hub page it appears as the "Chinese AI histories" section, without a priority label for readers. Preserve distinct organizations, researchers, sources and Chinese-language expressions. “Chinese AI” must not be collapsed into one actor or strategy; a shared discovery entry does not establish shared ownership, lineage or purpose.

## Next-phase priorities

The next major development phase prioritizes visible research usefulness:

1. A polished, immediately legible public-facing experience.
2. Researcher-friendly entry points beyond institutions: people, topics, documents and historical questions.
3. Deep flagship research paths, especially frontier-AI governance.
4. Exportable research starters with claims, primary and other source paths, relevant passages, qualifications, disagreements and open questions.
5. Continued corpus growth once these capabilities are useful.

Continue bounded claim additions where they answer concrete historical questions. Corpus growth must not substitute for a usable research product. Major work should make Selekoa materially deeper as documentary history or materially more useful to a researcher. Avoid generic infrastructure and repetitive audits without a concrete need; required editorial QA remains part of research work.

## Evidence and differentiation

Every substantive claim requires direct inspectable evidence appropriate to its exact proposition. Preserve temporal qualification, source versions, passages and locators, disagreement, retrieval limits, and the separate dimensions of derivation, resolution and review. Use the closest and strongest documentary source for the claim; primary status is not a truth ranking. Wikipedia and comparable tertiary/general-reference summaries are discovery aids, never evidence for an admitted claim. See the [source standard](editorial-policy.md#atomic-claims-and-sources).

Symfield, AI History Project, BehaviorGraph and Epoch overlap with parts of Selekoa; see the [dated adjacent-project assessment](adjacent-projects.md). A network graph establishes no novelty. Selekoa's intended differentiator is depth of documentary provenance, historical versioning, temporal qualification, explicit disagreement and uncertainty, and research usefulness. These are goals to demonstrate, not claims that adjacent projects lack them.

Epoch remains the stronger specialist resource for quantitative model and compute data. Reference it rather than imitate it; selective reuse requires attribution and exact version/terms records.

## Boundaries

Selekoa is not a comprehensive model database, benchmark tracker, chip catalog, generic company directory, news aggregator or replacement for specialist quantitative datasets. Proximity, chronology and correlation must never silently become influence.

The repository remains private and pre-release, maintained pseudonymously. A public-facing design priority does not authorize publication, deployment, visibility changes or commercial packaging.
