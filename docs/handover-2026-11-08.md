## How the work ran this week (and what to keep)

- **Roles:** Claude prepares packs; Codex (run locally by the maintainer) audits them blind and does archive retrieval. Codex prompts say plainly what the auditor must not open before its answers are committed (`review/decisions/answers-2026-11-05.md`).
- **Session size:** one large session per finished thing (records, schema and page in one release) costs less than many small ones; sessions continue after the five-hour limit on the maintainer's standing OK. Only one data branch at a time; an interface branch may run beside it.
- **Decisions:** sheets mark each item "Quick: OK the recommendation" or "Needs your judgement"; answers are filed in `review/decisions/answers-<date>.md` before any session acts on them.
- **Permissions:** the automatic permission check refuses attaching `selekoa/selekoa`, pushing to it, and unattended merges. Public syncs need the maintainer's approval in the sync session; merges need "merge N".
- **Sources:** an unauthorised scan of a copyrighted work is never cited; a record resting on the other family's reading of an authorised copy says so.
