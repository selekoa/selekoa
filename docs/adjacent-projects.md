## Strategic relationship

Symfield, AI History Project, BehaviorGraph and Epoch overlap with parts of Selekoa. Having a network graph is not a novelty claim or competitive objective. Selekoa's intended differentiator is depth of documentary provenance, historical versioning, temporal qualification, explicit disagreement and uncertainty, and research usefulness. These are aims to demonstrate, not assertions that other projects lack them.

The durable asset is the evidence-backed historical corpus and its research semantics. Dossiers, person views, document/version histories, timelines, source inspection, research starters and exports may serve a question better than a graph. Reduce or omit graph functionality if it does not improve historical understanding; do not preserve it solely for visual competition.

The assessments below retain their original inspection dates and factual scope; this strategy update is not a new audit of adjacent products.

## BehaviorGraph / Silicon Valley AI Leadership Map

[Map](https://map.behaviorgraph.com/) · [About](https://map.behaviorgraph.com/about-map/). Reachable. The leadership network and companion research bring people, organisations and career connections together, supporting exploration of paths and organisational ties. That overlaps substantially with Selekoa's people layer.

Selekoa's emphasis is the exact scoped claim behind each relationship, its documentary passage, time semantics and review state. Learn from accessible path exploration without treating every tie as influence or importing a leadership network wholesale.

## AI History Project

[Homepage](https://aihistoryproject.org/) · [About](https://aihistoryproject.org/about). Re-inspected 2026-11-08, replacing the 2026-09-19 description. The site is an explainer of technical concepts, people and organisations arranged by decade from 1936 to 2024: 124 concepts, 161 people and 124 organisations on its listing pages that day, with a knowledge graph, visualisations and a news feed. Each item links to its Wikipedia article. Its about page says the timeline entries "were researched and drafted with AI assistance from public sources, then reviewed and published under human editorial control", and asks readers to check the Wikipedia links.

Not listed on the About page. It is maintained and free, but it sends readers to Wikipedia rather than to sources, so it fails the transparency requirement of the inclusion bar for a recommendation. Both projects connect people and ideas through time; Selekoa concentrates on a versioned documentary record with claim-level evidence.

## Symfield AI Lineage Network

[AI Lineage Network](https://www.symfield.ai/ai-lineage/) · [Common Soil](https://www.symfield.ai/common-soil-how-frontier-ai-reproduces-itself/). Reachable; both inspected. This is a close adjacent project, not merely a visual reference: it presents typed, dated, sourced relationships, uncertainty notes and paths across people, methods, models, money, machines, data and evaluation. Its treatment distinguishes personnel paths from corporate descent.

There is substantial overlap with Selekoa's institutional and intellectual ambitions. Selekoa should make its own evidence/version inspection, separate derivation/resolution/review dimensions, documentary governance revisions and qualified transaction semantics useful in practice. Those are emphases, not a claim that Symfield lacks provenance. Do not import its interpretive conclusions as independently established facts.

## Epoch AI

[AI Models explorer](https://epoch.ai/data/ai-models). Reachable. Listed on the About page (see below). Measurements, filters, uncertainty information, documentation and official downloads make it the appropriate adjacent resource for quantitative model and compute questions. The inspected page describes attribution-based Creative Commons reuse; verify the exact dataset/version's terms before reuse rather than generalising that statement.

Epoch remains the stronger specialist resource for quantitative model and compute data; reference it rather than imitate it. Selekoa should selectively reference or enrich historically consequential records, retaining upstream IDs, uncertainty, version and attribution. It should not become a competing model/compute catalogue.

## AISafety.world / AI Safety map

[AISafety.world](https://aisafety.world/) redirects to the reachable [AI Safety map](https://aisafety.com/map). The inspected directory uses search and categories to help readers find organisations, programmes and resources. It is useful for ecosystem discovery.

Selekoa overlaps in some organisations and governance topics, but records scoped historical propositions rather than directory membership. Inclusion in such a map is not evidence of an individual's ideology, employment or affiliation. Use it for discovery, followed by direct source inspection.

## CivLab

[CivLab's US government graph](https://graph.civlab.org/us). Retained inspection dated 2026-09-17; not re-audited in this increment. It combined institutional structure, entity-linked news and dated changes, separating organisations, positions and people and exposing sources for changes.

Useful lessons are multiple entry points, explicit change and focus. Selekoa does not copy its hierarchy, visual structure or analytical assumptions; heterogeneous documentary claims require their own temporal and evidence semantics. No CivLab data imported.

## Resources listed on the About page

Inspected 2026-11-08 by direct fetch; the reasons against the inclusion bar are in `review/where-else-to-look-2026-11-08.md`. Inclusion bar: substantial free public content, updated within the last year, transparent about sources or methods, and answering a question Selekoa does not try to answer.

- [Epoch AI data](https://epoch.ai/data): databases of AI models (over 3,700 stated), benchmarks, data centres, chip sales and companies, with documentation and downloads; data pages updated 2026-11-06 and 2026-11-08. Data stated as reusable with credit under a Creative Commons Attribution licence.
- [METR research](https://metr.org/research): evaluations of frontier models' autonomous capabilities, task-completion time-horizon estimates and risk assessments; latest item dated 2026-11-05.
- [Stanford AI Index](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index): an annual report of indicators across research, technical performance, economy, policy and public opinion, with public data; 2026 edition released 2026-04.
- [OECD.AI Policy Navigator](https://oecd.ai/en/dashboards/overview): national and international AI policies and initiatives from more than 80 jurisdictions and organisations, by its own statement.
- [AI Incident Database](https://incidentdatabase.ai/): incidents in which AI systems caused or nearly caused harm, each with linked reports, under a published working definition; reports added 2026-11-07.
- [MIT AI Risk Repository](https://airisk.mit.edu/): over 1,700 risks drawn from published frameworks and classified by cause and domain, with governance and incident views; updates dated 2026-04 and 2026-06. CC BY 4.0.
- [CSET](https://cset.georgetown.edu/) and its [Emerging Technology Observatory](https://eto.tech/): reports, data briefs and English translations of foreign, mostly Chinese, policy documents; ETO tools on AI research, patents and investment by country and on chip supply chains.

Considered and not listed on 2026-11-08: Our World in Data's AI pages (the inspected chart last updated 2025-03-12; its AI charts reprocess Epoch AI and AI Index data), the AI History Project (above) and SemiAnalysis (core research behind institutional subscriptions).
