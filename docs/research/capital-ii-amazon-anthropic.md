# Capital II dossier — Amazon / Anthropic / AWS

Selected for staged investment, provider priority and explicit future conditions. Amazon and AWS remain separate graph entities. Research checked 2026-09-25; the corridor is not a closed graph cycle.

The [CMA's decision](https://assets.publishing.service.gov.uk/media/6710ba44e84ae1fd8592f52c/Full_text_decision.pdf), issued September 27, 2024 and published October 17, p. 4 paragraph 12(a), describes the initial investment using two convertible notes. Existing `amazon-anthropic-initial-2023` ($1.25 billion) and `amazon-anthropic-tranche-2024` ($2.75 billion) are stages of the initial $4 billion, not additions to the whole `amazon-investment-2023` ceiling. Conversion conditions do not establish that equity conversion occurred.

Paragraph 12(b) supports new `capital-ii-anthropic-aws-nonexclusive-2024`: compute provision is non-exclusive although AWS is primary. The provider share is redacted. Paragraph 12(c) separately addresses model distribution. The decision's jurisdictional conclusion (p. 2, paragraph 7) does not determine material influence; do not import the different Google decision's conclusion.

The [April 20, 2026 Amazon announcement](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/amazon-invests-additional-5-billion-anthropic-ai), investment and commercial-expansion paragraphs, ties the potential further investment to commercial milestones. This is an explicit condition, not merely inferred reciprocity. Public wording does not disclose all milestones or trace the original investment dollars into particular AWS invoices. Company statements about expected capacity remain prospective.

The [FTC report](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/p246201_aipartnerships6breport_redacted_0.pdf), §4.3, discusses investment-linked spending; see the attribution and redaction limits in [Capital II](../capital-loops.md). Do not convert its public-reporting table or anonymized internal observations into additional named contract clauses.
