Paragraph 16(d) addresses distribution on Vertex AI separately. Paragraphs 28–30, pp. 9–10, discuss other suppliers, distribution and financing. The CMA did not find Google's supplier/financing role conferred material influence in this assessment. Non-exclusivity alone is not sufficient to decide material influence. The finding is not a guarantee against economic dependence, switching costs or future changes.

`google-anthropic-convertible-debt-2024` → `anthropic-google-nonexclusive-2024` closes a directed entity cycle at the decision's snapshot date. It does not trace financing proceeds to Google invoices. The equity snapshot is earlier; no continuous ownership interval is manufactured to make another simultaneous cycle.

Later existing `anthropic-google-tpu-plan-2025` is prospective. `anthropic-google-capacity-2026` and `anthropic-broadcom-capacity-2026` describe two counterparties to one joint agreement, with capacity expected from 2027; they are not additive bilateral quantities. Do not use those later plans to fill the CMA's redacted 2024 amount or duration.

The [FTC discussion](https://www.ftc.gov/system/files/ftc_gov/pdf/p246201_aipartnerships6breport_redacted_0.pdf), §4.3, provides attributed linkage analysis, but anonymized passages cannot resolve these redactions. The application adds no cash-flow or dependency predicate. Human review should confirm the new commitment's narrow scope and absence of an invented minimum spend.
