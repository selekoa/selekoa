The issuer describes contra-revenue accounting and says treatment was still being evaluated. These facts support contractual linkage, not a finding of artificial revenue. Stock issuance remains sourced research prose; no unsuitable investment edge is added.

No default, exercised termination, foreclosure, completed SPV borrowing or traced return of cash is established. Do not apply March remedies automatically to May orders or assign company-wide take-or-pay statistics to this customer. “Additional” distinguishes the May agreement, but this increment calculates no combined spend or net exposure. All application edges point OpenAI → CoreWeave; their coexistence is not a directed cycle. Human inspection should focus on the exact conditional rights and upper-bound amount.
