The expanded selection corrects the earlier principal omission, while the separate leadership group avoids equating executive office with founding participation. The remaining risks are institutional self-description, uneven documentation dates, and selective context: three people's prior roles and two publication topics cannot represent every founder's background. The maintainer sheet asks for source and framing inspection without recording acceptance.

The focused checks cover founding completeness, exact selection identity, original object identity, passage reference integrity, temporal precision, all evidence routes, regrouped bookmarks and keyboard return. Browser checks cover production preview in Edge, both themes, mobile and desktop, reduced motion, accessibility, overflow and console errors. Generated browser output stays ignored. Existing editorial working packs are not regenerated or overwritten.

The reading supplies historical distinctions that previously had to be reconstructed from individual cards, and keeps eleven contextual records optional. Compact principal rows offset the four additional prominent attributions. This is a design assessment, not evidence from a comparative reader study: maintainer inspection should still test comprehension and perceived effort.
