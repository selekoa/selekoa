# Person profiles — future product direction

Every person entity now has a page (`#/person/PERSON_ID`): stored description and identity sources, a documented path of role, founding, launch and departure records in date order with an Undated group, works authored, explicit credits, governance-topic records, a computed “What this record cannot tell you” list, a secondary neighbourhood map and a research-starter export. It is computed from claims by fixed rules; see [research references](research-references.md#person-document-and-question-pages). The rest of this note remains the direction for curated profiles: a future collection might cover 50–100 historically consequential people. Selection should explain institutional or intellectual history, not satisfy a fame quota.

A profile should combine a name and concise sourced orientation with a chronological institutional path, roles and dates, co-founding events, departures, key papers/documents, documented intellectual links, governance responsibilities, relevant capital roles, a graph neighbourhood and an evidence panel.

Assemble these sections from structured claims. Each timeline item must retain its exact proposition, time kind and precision, qualification, and one-click evidence. A short editorial introduction could later be separately sourced and human-reviewed. Avoid an authoritative free-text biography that drifts away from the underlying records.

Snapshots are not tenures. Missing end dates are unknown, not continuing appointments. Undated retrospective roles belong in a clearly marked section rather than an invented sequence. Simultaneous affiliations can coexist. Printed author affiliation does not establish employment; advisory, board and employee roles must remain distinguishable. Announcement dates and effective departure dates must remain separate.

Readers may reconstruct paths across institutions, but the system must not invent a transfer, mentorship, friendship or influence edge from adjacent roles or co-authorship. Predecessor and combined institutions retain separate identities.

Use the [current positioning](project-positioning.md) and [canonical visual direction](../design/visual-language-v2.md). Preserve an accessible list equivalent, coherent filters and timeline, individually selectable parallel claims, reduced-motion support and the Evidence Inspector. Dense full-graph views are not a substitute for focused histories.
