# AI and biosecurity, part 1: admission, 2026-10-30

Part 1 of the admission of the candidate pack in [`research/ai-bio/`](../research/ai-bio/README.md): the frameworks, synthesis-screening and government-measures records, all on existing predicates. It follows the maintainer's decisions of 2026-10-28, which adopt every recommended answer of the [pack report](ai-bio-pack-2026-10-28.md), and the corrections of the [cross-family check](../research/ai-bio/cross-family-check-2026-10-28.md). This branch brings in the pack and the check's report and JSON from `codex/ai-bio-check`, which contains the pack branch.

- **Script:** [`scripts/ai-bio-admission.ts`](../scripts/ai-bio-admission.ts) builds the records from the pack and the authored [map](ai-bio-admission-map-2026-10-30.json). `--verify` matches every quotation in a fresh retrieval, `--part 1` writes the records and `--part 1 --check` rebuilds them and compares them with the stored data.
- **Before-snapshot:** [`ai-bio-admission-1-before-2026-10-30.json`](ai-bio-admission-1-before-2026-10-30.json), first in the chain in `src/editorial/historical-views.ts`, before the archive fixes' snapshot. It was regenerated by the script from the new base (`main` at b723a0c, v0.14.1) after `main` was merged in (below). Part 1 changes no existing row.
- **Fresh retrieval at admission:** all 88 pack sources and four further texts were retrieved again on 2026-10-30 into the ignored cache, and all 243 quotations of both parts matched ([`admission-verification-2026-10-30.json`](../research/ai-bio/admission-verification-2026-10-30.json)). The Internet Archive refused this environment throughout, as it did the pack.
- **Schema:** unchanged (2.21). Every record uses an existing predicate and existing attributes.
- **Editorial state:** every record is a seed. No evidence is human-reviewed and no human translation is claimed.
- **Hazard rule:** every record stays at the policy and evaluation level. No technical, procedural or screening-criterion content is recorded, and where a correction asked for a material limitation it is stated at that level only.

## Admission's own changes

- **Date bases.** A provision is dated by one of its expression's own dates. Executive Order 14110's and 14409's provisions move from the signature date to the Federal Register publication date, with the signature in the note, as the frameworks-to-law admission did. The OSTP framework's September 2024 records carry the `revision` basis.
- **Attributes the schema places elsewhere.** The activity stage on the two UK implementation-report records and the change-evidence mode on the HHS supersession are dropped; each qualification already states what they carried, and the schema allows those fields only on evaluation announcements and version-change records.
- **Provision keys** are lower-cased slugs, as the schema requires.
- **Document families.** The three HHS guidance editions share one family under the Department, so that the 2023 supersession of the 2010 guidance holds; the component each notice names is recorded by its publication record. The two bills of the same proposed act are separate families, because a family has one publisher identity.
- **Documents without their own dates** (the IGSC member directory and the House bill as introduced) are admitted without version metadata, which the schema requires to carry at least one date.
- **Sources.** The pack's own source for Executive Order 14409 is replaced by the dataset's. Unchecked capture addresses are not recorded: the pack marks them unchecked, and no record rests on one.
- **Reader text.** Record IDs in the pack's wording became "recorded separately".
- **Research script.** `research/ai-bio/build.mjs` had a duplicate `case` label, which fails the repository's lint. The dead second label was removed; the first match governed, so the brief it generates is unchanged.

## Held

23 pack records are not admitted. Each is listed with its reason in the map's `hold` block.

| Held                                               | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         |
| -------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 18 provision records resting on a paraphrase alone | The schema requires a provision record to carry a verbatim quotation of at most 50 words from the cited expression. The pack paraphrases where the per-source quotation budget is spent (RSP v1.0, v3.0 and FSF v3.1 are each within a few words of their limit), where the edition's PDF text layer is broken, and where the quotable sentence descends below the policy level. Decision 7 accepted the paraphrases; the schema's provision rule does not. |
| 4 provision records of undated served pages        | The schema dates every provision record by one of its expression's own dates, with a basis. Decision 8 kept these as snapshots, which the provision rule does not allow.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    |
| `ab-scr-igsc-hsp-v31-customer-screening`           | The check's register review found conditional customer-screening and eligibility detail beyond a general governance obligation. Narrowing it would either reproduce that detail or leave the record without its required quotation. The locator correction (§7.1 is on PDF p. 13) is recorded in the map.                                                                                                                                                   |

The 18 are: the RSP v1.0 tiered-access exception; the Preparedness beta high level; three Preparedness v2 bio-chem records; two FSF v3.0 records; the FSF v3.1 early-warning evaluations; two RSP v3.0 records and one each for v3.1, v3.2, v3.3 and v3.4; the Microsoft February 2026 critical threshold; two Frontier Governance Framework records; and the IGSC revision history.
