- **Roles.** Audit and citable-copies reads: OpenAI Codex (the checking family). Adjudication: a Claude session (the preparing family). Machine agreement is not human review; every record stays a seed.
- **Rule applied.** Where the preparer agrees with the checker, the correction is entered as `agreed`. Where the preparer disagrees on a stance, status, voice or headline, the item is held for the maintainer and nothing is applied on the preparer's say (editorial policy, cross-family check). Widened records that the checker did not see are entered as `agreed` only where they share the finding's exact error and the same evidence settles them; otherwise they are held together.
- **Map format.** Each entry names its section, the finding or pattern it answers (`ref`), its status (`agreed`, `held-quick`, `held-judgement`), the operation (`set`, `add`, `remove`), the record, the field path, the value now in `data/` (`old`) and the new value, with the reason. An `unset` entry removes a field. An entry marked `oldAfterEarlierEntry` follows an earlier entry on the same field. The data session should stop on any `old` that no longer matches.
