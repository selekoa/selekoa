# Capital and compute: Audit B's RESEARCH MORE items, resolved 2026-10-15

Audit B of the capital and compute pack ([`capital-compute-review.md`](capital-compute-review.md)) left 13 claims at RESEARCH MORE: 05, 06, 11, 12, 13, 19, 20, 21, 22, 23, 28, 29 and 30. Its reasons were missing primary-source inspection, not contrary evidence. This pass retrieved the cited primary source for each claim, located the passage, and checked the claim's wording, amount, bound, stage, date and qualification against it.

**Result:** all 13 claims are supported by the passage of the cited primary source. No claim's proposition, amount, bound, stage, date, qualification, resolution or editorial state changed. Each evidence record now carries a verbatim quotation of the passage in place of the earlier paraphrase, a more precise locator where needed, the check date 2026-10-15 and a note on how the page was retrieved. Each of the six sources records its approximate length, as the quotation limits require. The annotated pack and its two audit transcripts are unchanged.

**What this is not.** The check was made by the same agent that made the change, with the earlier pack open. It is not a fresh-context audit, it is not human source review, and it accepts nothing: every claim stays a seed (`Not reviewed by an editor`) and every evidence record stays `machine_checked`. A company announcement documents what the company announced. It does not show that money was paid, capacity delivered or a contract kept.
