# AI in biology: admission, 2026-10-31

Admission of the candidate pack in [`research/ai-in-biology/`](../research/ai-in-biology/README.md), in two parts. It follows the maintainer's decisions of 2026-10-30, which adopt every recommended answer of the [pack report](ai-in-biology-pack-2026-10-30.md) and the nine points of its adjudication, and the corrections of the [cross-family check](../research/ai-in-biology/cross-family-check-2026-10-30.md). This branch brings in the pack, the check's report and JSON from `codex/ai-in-biology-check`, and the review note with the decisions from `ai-in-biology-pack`.

- **Script:** [`scripts/ai-in-biology-admission.ts`](../scripts/ai-in-biology-admission.ts) builds every record from the pack and the authored [map](ai-in-biology-admission-map-2026-10-31.json). `--part 1` and `--part 2` write the records; `--part N --check` rebuilds them and compares them with the stored data. A part first removes the rows its before-snapshot lists as added and restores the rows it changed, so it runs again on the same base or a new one. Part 1 reruns only on a dataset without part 2: part 2 is first reverted by its before-snapshot (its added rows removed, its changed rows and the metadata restored), then part 1 and part 2 are written again.
- **Before-snapshots:** [`ai-in-biology-admission-1-before-2026-10-31.json`](ai-in-biology-admission-1-before-2026-10-31.json) and [`ai-in-biology-admission-2-before-2026-10-31.json`](ai-in-biology-admission-2-before-2026-10-31.json), part 2's first and part 1's second in the chain in `src/editorial/historical-views.ts`. Base: branch `people-admission` (v0.15.1), the previous admission of the 2026-10-31 chain; each part's snapshot was regenerated from that base by the script after the review corrections (part 1 at `73b6910`, part 2 at `73b6910`).
- **Fresh retrieval at admission:** none of the pack's 108 sources was retrieved again; every pack quotation rests on the pack's quotation check of 2026-10-30 (360 quotations, no problem) and, for the 120 items it covered, on the cross-family check. Three texts were retrieved directly on 2026-10-31 for the decisions: Roche's Annual Report 2021, the PubMed record of Bray (1990) and Berg's release of 21 November 2013. Each returned the bytes the check reported or carries its own hash, and each added quotation matched. The Internet Archive is not reachable from this environment; seven captures the check opened are recorded as sources of their own, with paraphrases of what the check reported.
- **Editorial state:** every record is a seed, every evidence item `machine_checked`. No evidence is human-reviewed and no human translation is claimed; four Chinese quotations carry an English machine translation in their notes, marked as not verified by a person.
- **Hazard rule:** records stay at the institutional, publication and claim level. The pack's hazard notes on ESM3, Evo, Evo 2, RFdiffusion, AlphaProteo and protein-design texts are kept in the qualifications.

## Schema 2.23 (part 2)

- **`partnership_announced`** (`src/domain/predicates.ts`): organisation → organisation, with `activityStage: announced`, dated by its announcement or unknown, explicit and supported by a primary passage. An "up to" amount is refused by validation.
- **`award_announced`**: awarding body (organisation or public body) → person, dated by its announcement, with no amount. The pack's `award`, `share` and `citation` attributes are not added to the schema: the prize, the share and the citation are in the statement and the quoted passage.
- `partnership_announced` joins the predicates that may keep the stage `announced`; both predicates join the map's relation families (`src/ui/mapGrammar.ts`). Recorded in [`docs/ontology.md`](../docs/ontology.md) and [`docs/dataset-format.md`](../docs/dataset-format.md). No file, column or top-level release field changes, so the release format stays 1.8.

## Admission's own changes

- **Documents without version metadata.** The pack's documents are admitted without `documentVersion`: its families group a company's releases or a model's papers, which are not editions of one text. Each document is dated by its source's `publicationDate`, taken from the pack's own date for it (publication, posting, announcement or release, in that order).
- **Provisions of releases.** A stated provision is dated by one of its expression's dates, with a basis other than announcement. The 12 provision records of releases and filings dated by announcement carry the basis `publication`, with a sentence in the note saying that the release's date of publication is its date of announcement.
- **Attributes the schema has no place for:** `statedStart` on the HHMI role (its "2000-Present" stays in the statement and note) and the award attributes (above).
- **Round totals** are numbers in their currency, as the schema requires of `announced_funding`.
- **Usual names.** Validation requires one record per proposition to hold the earliest located use of a usual name where any record's evidence uses one. Lyu et al. (2024) holds it for "structure-based drug design" (P1) and Tomita (2001) for "virtual cell" (P2).
- **Subtypes** follow the closed lists: the pack's "Filing" becomes "Regulatory filing", "Venture creation firm" becomes "Investor", "Research organisation" becomes "Research institute", and the Royal Swedish Academy of Sciences is a "Nonprofit" organisation.
- **Sort names** for five people whose family name comes first (Wen Shuhao, Ma Jian, Lai Lipeng, Liu Wei) or whose name carries a Chinese form (Robin Li).
- **Reader text.** Record IDs became "recorded separately"; references to the pack's passes, its integrator and its brief were reworded in the map's `phrases`; a pointer to a held record says that the record is not admitted.
- **One row per retrieved page.** The map's `sameAs` sends a pack source key to the row of another key that retrieved the same page; the script builds that row once (four Nature articles).
- **Research scripts.** Two lint errors in the pack's scripts were fixed without changing their output: a dead duplicate `case 'introduced'` in `build.mjs` (the first label governs, as in the AI and biosecurity pack) and an unused loop variable in `integrate.mjs`. No other pack artefact was edited.

## Still open

1. **The 20 provisions of undated pages.** Holding them follows the AI and biosecurity decision; a later decision could admit current statements of organisations under another predicate or date.
2. **P4 and P5** carry no earliest located text until their criteria are adjudicated, with the check's candidates named.
3. **A collection page for institutions, papers and people.** "AI in science" orders the claims strand. A page that lists a field's institutions, models and people would be a new collection kind, with its own page and release kind; adding the part 1 records to a collection's computed contents would also enlarge the collection index every page loads.
4. **Hub placement (question 1), deferred.** The maintainer adopted Institutions & people as the home hub of the institutions, models and people strands. Only the people strand reaches that hub, through People A–Z. Placing the institutions and models strands there is deferred: the hub lists dossiers of core institutions and Chinese AI histories, and a list of a field's institutions and models is the new collection kind of item 3, which this admission does not add.
5. **Publisher records (question 8), not applied.** Question 8 adopts replacing the PubMed and PubMed Central records with the publisher's article where the checker or a later thread can read it. For ESM-2, ESM3, Evo and RoseTTAFold the check read the publisher's pages in Internet Archive captures; the admission could not retrieve those captures, and adding them would add only paraphrases of the check's report, so the records still rest on PubMed, PubMed Central and the Arc Institute page, and their notes still say science.org refused retrieval. Their dates already match the publisher's online dates (16 March 2023, 16 January 2025, 15 November 2024, 15 July 2021). For RoseTTAFold the check also reports an issue date of 20 August 2021 and a print date of 19 August on the publisher's page, which disagree.
6. **The two held provisions of the ESM3 post.** They could be admitted at the post's date with the attestation note its other provisions carry, or the admitted two could be held with them.
