## Method

**Archive links (decision 1).** A source gains `archivedUrl` and `archivedAt` when it had no archive copy and the check read a capture whose document identity it confirmed: the cited capture, one found through the Wayback Machine's CDX index, or one the check saved with Save Page Now and then read back. The URL is the capture the check used (`capture.url` in the report, the replay form without `id_`), and `archivedAt` is the day of its timestamp, as the schema requires. Sources whose quotations differed only in typography also gain the link: the capture shows the document. A capture shows only what the archive held on its date; for 429 of the 532 links that is a 2026 capture, later than the source's publication, and some were saved during the check itself. A cited capture is replaced only where the check shows that it failed (four sources); where the check merely followed the cited capture's own redirect, the cited capture is kept. Captures of X posts were verified from the raw replay's structured post data; five Usenet messages are members of a monthly archive file, which is the capture. No source's `version` text was changed; this record is the record of the method.

**Quotations (decision 2).** A stored quotation is corrected to the capture's text where the difference is the source's own typography or wording and the proposition is unchanged. For 1:1 differences of quotation marks, apostrophes and commas, the script replaces only those characters and refuses anything else; editorial elision marks (`[...]`) stay as stored, since they are Selekoa's notation, not the source's. Four corrections were written by hand from the report (the EU news item's opening word, the SaferAI headline's capitalisation, the template's digit-grouping space and the six TC260 full-width commas, the last by the same 1:1 rule). Each corrected record's `checkedAt` is the day the check read the capture, and its note says what changed and on what basis. A record's `retrievalRoute` is kept: it says how the passage was first read, and none of the schema's routes describes an archive read (the precedent of the [archive admissions of 2026-10-26](archive-admissions-2026-10-26.md)); where one is set, the note says it is that of the earlier check.

**Locators (decision 3).** One: the check inspected the Solomonoff passage visually on PDF page 5.

**Canonical addresses (decision 5).** 15 of the 16 same-document moves of the link-health report, `source-us-ai-action-plan-release-2025` among them, now give the address the old one redirects to. `evidenceUrl` is unchanged throughout: it is the address of the expression that was inspected. `source-rsp-3-0` keeps its policy page address (maintainer's decision 3, below): the page redirects to a content-hashed PDF address, which is fragile, and its `evidenceUrl`, the same page address, still serves the PDF that was inspected.

The first run also held a human-reviewed passage, a capture change and every fix to a passage that bears a proposition collection's headline role (earliest located, earliest located tier 2, earliest located use of the name, most developed), under the override rule, with any record that quotes the same passage of the same source.
