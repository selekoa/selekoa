# Citation author names and string clean-up, 2026-10-15

Schema 2.14 adds an optional `authorNames` field to sources: one structured name per credited author, in the order of `authors`. A person has `family`, `given` and, where printed, `suffix`; an organisation, a group or a pseudonym is a `literal` and is never split. CSL-JSON, BibTeX, RIS and Copy APA use it when present and fall back to the earlier person-record mapping otherwise. The field is a cataloguing aid for citation, not evidence about anyone's name. The rows as they were are in [`citation-authors-before-2026-10-15.json`](citation-authors-before-2026-10-15.json). No title, date, URL, claim, evidence record or review state changed.

Splitting rule: the last word is the family name; lower-case particles (`de`, `van`, `van den`, `de Las`) and a trailing `Jr.` stay with it; initials and middle names stay in the given name, as credited. Where a person record exists, the split agrees with its `sortName` except for two credited forms (`Christopher Olah`, `Diederik P. Kingma`), which keep the given name as printed. Romanised Chinese names in arXiv listings are given-first and split that way; `于丽丽` is split as family `于`, given `丽丽`. Role notes in parentheses, such as “(Working Group 1 Co-Chair)”, are left out of the structured name and kept in `authors`.
