# Second frontier-governance increment: machine audit, 2026-10-11

Scope: 25 claims, 53 new evidence records, 12 sources and 9 entities added for the question `frameworks-to-obligations`, and three changed rows (the Code of Practice and New York RAISE Act entities, and the New York signing-announcement claim, whose qualification was corrected and which gained one context evidence item). All records are machine-checked seeds, not editorially accepted. The rows changed are listed in `frontier-governance-2-before-2026-10-11.json`.

Added: Commission guidelines on general-purpose AI models (9 claims), the training-content template (2), the Transparency and Copyright chapters of the EU Code (3), the Signatory Taskforce Vademecum (1), a draft implementing regulation on evaluations and proceedings (2), the New York bill S8828 signed as Chapter 96 (5) and the Chinese Interim Measures on generative AI services (3).

## Method

The proposing session retrieved each document by direct HTTP, extracted the text (PDF text layer or page text) and matched every quotation mechanically against it, whitespace and line-break hyphenation normalised, before storing it. Three fresh-context subagents (Claude models, the same family as the proposer; model version not recorded by the harness) then received only the claims, locators, passages and the extracted texts, not the proposing pass's reasoning. They did not edit records.

- Auditor A: the 11 guidelines and template claims.
- Auditor B: the 14 claims on Code chapters, Taskforce, draft regulation, New York and China.
- Auditor C: the riskiest 8 claims (`eugl-gpai-indicative-criterion`, `eugl-enforcement-timing`, `eugl-guidelines-dated`, `ny-chapter96-signed`, `ny-ch96-effective-date`, `cn-genai-measures-security-assessment`, `cm-eu-code-taskforce`, `eudraft-proceedings-published`) plus a seeded random draw of three more (SHA-256 of the claim ID with the label `fg2-audit-2026-10-11`: `eutm-top-domain-names`, `eutm-summary-updates`, `eugl-fines-mitigating-factor`).

Coverage: every claim was checked by at least one auditor; 11 by two. All quotations were found word for word in the named source, in every auditor's check. Auditors worked from the extracted texts the proposing session supplied, not from independent downloads. Items added after the audit in response to it (the Code page sentence on the Vademecum, Article 2(1) first sentence of the draft, the paragraph (16) context paraphrase) were matched mechanically only, except the paraphrase.

No claim in this increment is contested or an assessment, so the policy's different-model check does not apply. No other model family was reachable. If one is wanted anyway, the passages that carry the most risk are: the English renderings of Chinese Articles 2 and 17 (`cn-genai-measures-scope`, `cn-genai-measures-security-assessment`); the 10^23 FLOP criterion, where the PDF text layer shows `1023` (`eugl-gpai-indicative-criterion`); the enforcement-timing statement (`eugl-enforcement-timing`); and the reading of the New York bill print as the signed chapter (`ny-chapter96-signed`, `ny-ch96-*`).
