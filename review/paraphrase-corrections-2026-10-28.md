# Paraphrase corrections of 2026-10-28

A blind check of all 287 framework-comparison paraphrases by a reviewer from a different model family ([report](../research/frontier-framework-comparison/codex-paraphrases-2026-10-28.md)) found 24 drifts and 3 wrong locators. In most of the drifts the paraphrase left out a condition, scope limit or threshold that only the cell's note recorded. [`apply.mjs`](../research/paraphrase-corrections-2026-10-28/apply.mjs) made every data change in one run. The 27 evidence records it changed, as they were, are in [`paraphrase-corrections-before-2026-10-28.json`](paraphrase-corrections-before-2026-10-28.json), the first entry in the chain of `src/editorial/historical-views.ts`. Patch release **v0.10.2**.

All records remain seeds. No person has reviewed them.

## Decision (maintainer, 2026-10-28)

Correct all 27. Each paraphrase stands alone with the conditions, scope and thresholds its passage states, with the same modal force. Correct the three RSP v2.x locators. For the OpenAI Frontier Governance Framework qualifier “as available and appropriate”, keep the source's ambiguity: do not resolve whether it attaches to the Safety Advisory Group or to external input. Change no cell status. Where a fix would need a status change or judgement beyond the paraphrase, hold it and list it.

## How

- **Sources of the new wording.** The corrections were written from the passages the check quoted and from the editions themselves. On 2026-10-28, `tools.mjs fetch` retrieved 20 of the 22 editions from their publisher addresses, each with the SHA-256 recorded in the pack. Two could not be retrieved. For Meta's Frontier AI Framework v1.1, the Internet Archive reset the connection and the publisher address returned HTTP 500. For xAI's framework of June 2026, the archive reset the connection and the publisher address returned HTTP 404. Their three corrections rest on the passages as the check quoted them from its own copies, whose hashes match the pack. Each evidence note says so.
- **Pack.** In `paraphrases.json`, each corrected entry has its new paraphrase and, where changed, a new locator with `locatorCorrected`, a revised `note` and a `paraphraseCheck` record. `pack.json` carries the same `paraphraseCheck` record on the pack entry; the pack's quotations and locators are unchanged. `render.mjs` now prints that record, and `pack.md` was re-rendered.
- **Data.** Each of the 27 evidence records has the new passage and locator, `checkedAt` 2026-10-28, and a note naming the cross-family check and what it found. Claims, statuses, qualifications and every other record are unchanged.
- **Entry notes.** Where a note only repeated what the paraphrase now states, that part was removed. The FSF v3.1 note, which said footnote 4 was “not part of the paraphrase”, now gives only the footnote's example. The cell notes (claim qualifications) were not changed, so some of them repeat a condition that the paraphrase now also gives.
