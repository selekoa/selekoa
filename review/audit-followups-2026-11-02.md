**How the line is shown.** Schema 2.28 adds `proposition.underRecheck`: a list
of withdrawn headlines (`earliest-located`, `earliest-located-contesting`,
`earliest-located-term`), each with a reason naming the finding and the date.
Validation expects no record to hold a withdrawn role (instead of exactly one),
and the role is not moved. The proposition page shows the headline's label with
"Under re-check (2026-11-02)" (so W03 reads "Earliest located: Under re-check
(2026-11-02)"), and the research starter writes the same line. A record that
holds the role is shown in preference to the line, so the line disappears once
a role is set; the re-check that sets it must also remove the entry, or
validation fails and names the proposition. The `withheld` mechanism of schema
2.16 was not reused: it covers only the articulation roles and shows "Not
assigned" with a reason, not a neutral line.
