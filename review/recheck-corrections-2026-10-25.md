## Departures from the rulings

1. **G09 (ruling 4) needed schema 2.16.** Validation required exactly one earliest located articulation in each tier that has articulations, and three tier-1 G09 articulations remain (Hanson's December 2000 paper, Nordhaus 2015, and Erdil and Besiroglu 2023 after ruling 1). Leaving the role unheld was therefore impossible under schema 2.15. Schema 2.16 adds `proposition.withheld`: an earliest located role left unassigned by a recorded decision, with its reason and date. Validation then expects no holder in that tier; the interface and the research starter say "Not assigned" with the reason. It is the only schema change. See [ontology](../docs/ontology.md#withheld-earliest-located-roles-schema-216).
2. **Release v0.7.0, not v0.6.1.** The [release format](../docs/dataset-format.md#versions) makes a release with added records or a higher schema version a minor release; a patch is for corrections without additions. This release adds 22 records and raises the schema to 2.16.
3. **RSP parity sentence (ruling 11) as a separate passage.** The quotation cap is 50 words and the table cell is 53, so the delay sentence could not join the existing quotation. It is quoted as a second passage of the same record.
4. **No 1952 claimed date on the Diebold records (rulings 7–8).** The condensation is dated by the hearing day; the book's copyright year appears only in the date note, so that nothing reads as dating the wording to 1952.
5. **Nothing to correct for Reuther's date (ruling 8).** No stored record carried 1955-10-15; that date occurred only in the same-family report. The new record is dated 1955-10-17.
