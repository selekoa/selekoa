## Method

- **Blind first.** For each proposition, a packet was built from the #35 data. It gave each record's text, stance, admitted date, statement, conditions and passages with locators and source URLs. It withheld tier, tier basis, roles, qualification and date notes. Tiers were judged blind for all 59 documents before the stored tiers were read. For each role, the holder's fit to the criterion and its date were judged from the sources, and earlier instances were searched for, before the comparison.
- **Who did it.** A first attempt to run the blind judgements in eight fresh-context agents was stopped before they returned results, so none were used. The re-check was done by one session, which had not prepared or admitted the packs. Blindness was partial. Before the judgements, this session had read the admission record (`economics-admission-2026-10-14.md`), which names some settled tiers (Hanson 1994) and open items (G06, M09). It had not read the packs, the stored tiers and roles, or the review notes. It then read the stored values and, for scope only, the mainstream pack's scope note.
- **Sources.** Re-retrieved on 2026-10-25 by direct HTTP: the NBER working papers w21547, w22252, w23928, w24001, w24243 and w32487; the Oxford Martin PDF of Frey and Osborne; Acemoglu's April 2024 MIT copy; Hanson's "Economic Growth Given Machine Intelligence", "If Uploads Come First" and "Is a Singularity Just Around the Corner?" (current author-hosted copies); the OCR text of the 1955 hearings (archive.org); the MIT FutureTech profile; the arXiv abstract page of 2309.11690; the NBER chapter c14005; two Harvard Business Review articles; and the LessWrong page used in #37. Quotations were matched after normalising whitespace, quotation marks and hyphenation.
- **Earlier instances.** For each role, earlier texts were looked for in the collection itself, including the later esoteric strand and the thought-spine records. Outside it, the search covered the same authors' earlier work, texts cited by the holder, and the hearing transcripts. An earlier instance is reported as a disagreement even where it lies outside a pack's searched scope, since earliest located means within the recorded searches and a later find is a correction.
- **Not done.** No item that needs web.archive.org was re-read: `needs-archive-check`. Tier-1 passages of records that hold no role were not re-quoted (the admission re-checked 271 of 282 on 2026-10-14).
- This is a machine check by the preparing model family. It is not a cross-family check and not editorial acceptance. Wording follows the editorial policy: "earliest located", never "first".
