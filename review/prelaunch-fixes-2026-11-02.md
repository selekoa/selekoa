## How each correction was checked

The audit's sample checkers, readers and page auditors (Claude family) proposed
the findings. Each group was then re-checked in a two-stage machine review
(Claude family, 2026-11-01): a fixer re-read the record and, where the finding
concerned a source, the source itself, and proposed exact patches; an
independent skeptic re-read the same record and source and accepted, amended or
rejected each patch, defaulting to rejection when it could not confirm. Of 125
proposed patches, 115 were accepted and 10 amended; none was rejected. Three
were then amended again when the unit tests showed a page description shorter
than the site's minimum and two sub-round labels that read as internal codes;
these are marked below. Where a fixer re-read a claim's supporting passage, that
evidence record's check date is 2026-11-01 and its note says so; locators
corrected from a re-read source likewise. This is a same-family check: no
cross-family model reviewed these corrections, and none of them changes an
earliest-located role, a resolution or an assessment.

Sources that could not be read here: web.archive.org and openai.com refuse this
container. Where a correction rests on an archive capture, it rests on the
record of the earlier cross-family check, and says so.
