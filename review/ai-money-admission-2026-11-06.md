## Schema 2.33

- `attributes.thought.voice`: `institution`, `official`, `staff`, `committee`; every proposition record of one text gives the same voice. Needed because decision 3a shows the earliest located institutional text beside a headline, which the page can only derive if each record carries its text's voice; the pack plan's alternative (voice in the statement only) could not drive the page. An optional nested value, so the release format stays 1.10.
- The relay rule (a relay never holds earliest located, and its date does not decide) applies where records carry a voice, as it did in tiered collections; decision 6a.
- `launched` admits a public body as subject and object, for held item 6 (a government launching an institute). The smallest change that records the chair's statement as a launch, as the recommendation asked.
- Documented in `docs/dataset-format.md` and `docs/ontology.md`.

## The page

`/propositions/ai-and-money`, on the Ideas hub with the other proposition collections, with a CSV at `/propositions/ai-and-money.csv`.

- **Records:** each row's kind line names its voice ("Institution’s own text", "Speech or remarks", "Staff paper", "Committee report") beside its stance and roles; the evidence drawer shows the disclaimer as context.
- **Headlines:** "Earliest located" (with its voice), and, where a speech, a staff paper or a committee report holds it, "Earliest located institutional text", derived from the records (`earliestInstitution` in `src/domain/propositions.ts`).
- **Events:** "Institutional events", an ordered list in date order: stage ("Announced", "Stated as under way", "Stated as done"), body, date, statement, each opening its evidence. A list, so it is the same without JavaScript and with a keyboard.
- **CSV:** one row per record (code, proposition, stance, roles, voice, register, text, dates, statement, document and record addresses) and one per event (`row_kind`), from the same view (`src/domain/propositions-csv.ts`).
- **Code:** the page is the existing propositions page, loaded lazily; the collection's text is in `src/reading/ai-and-money.ts`, loaded only by its page; shared code gains its frame. Reader text checked against `docs/style-guide.md`; the self-check patterns find nothing in it.
