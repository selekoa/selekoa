# Decision sheet: Antecedent texts (machine intelligence before 1965)

The decision that matters most is **1, whether to admit the two new propositions** ("A machine can only do what it is ordered to do" and "A machine could think") as a new collection.

### 1. Admit the two new propositions

U01 "A machine can only do what it is ordered to do" (earliest located: Lovelace 1843; earliest contesting: Turing 1947; the name "Lady Lovelace's objection" first located in Turing 1950). U02 "A machine could think" (earliest located: _Erewhon_ 1872, fiction; earliest non-fiction: Turing 1947; no contesting text yet).

- **a.** Yes, after the cross-family check, in a new collection "Antecedent texts: machine intelligence before 1965".
- **b.** Admit the records on existing propositions only; no new propositions or collection.
- **c.** Not now.

_Recommendation: a._ Both ideas recur across the period and have no home in the dataset.

- a: a new collection page with two propositions and their texts.
- b: the texts appear only in existing collections.
- c: nothing changes for readers.

### 2. _Erewhon_'s civil war and W04

W04 is "Conflict over building superhuman machines": disagreement over building superhuman machine intelligence divides people into opposed groups and could lead to war. _Erewhon_ (1872) describes a civil war over machines that are not described as superhuman intelligence.

- **a.** Keep it as context.
- **b.** Count it as stating W04 (it would become the earliest text, before de Garis 1989).

_Recommendation: a._ Counting it would stretch the criterion.

- a: _Erewhon_ appears on W04 as context only.
- b: W04's headline moves to 1872.

### 3. A third proposition: the technological singularity

Draft criterion: "The acceleration of technological change is approaching a point, called a singularity or described as a discontinuity, beyond which human affairs as they have been known could not continue or could not be foreseen."

- **a.** Hold until Ulam's 1958 memoir of von Neumann is retrieved.
- **b.** Add it now, with Vinge (1983, 1993, already in the dataset) as the texts that state it.
- **c.** Do not add it.

_Recommendation: a._ The 1958 text is the expected earliest candidate; adding the proposition without it would set a headline likely to move.

- a: no change yet.
- b: a third proposition with a 1983 headline.
- c: the idea stays without its own proposition.
