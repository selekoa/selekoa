### 5. C1 "Behavioural tests as evidence": which earlier text heads it

Needs your judgement: the checker and the preparer disagree on an earliest-located headline.

C1's criterion is "an explicit claim that apparent capability, understanding or awareness shown in a behavioural test, such as the imitation game, is weak or misleading evidence of what the system is". Both families agree that Turing's "Heretical Theory" (about 1951) is no longer the earliest text found. Jefferson's oration (published 1949-06-25) says a machine that "typewrites its answers" may seem to have "learned to write" when it is doing "no more than telegraphic systems do already".

- **a.** Jefferson 1949 heads C1. Descartes 1637 is context. (Preparer: Descartes describes machines' performances, not a test, and holds that two behavioural tests would reliably tell machines from people.)
- **b.** Descartes 1637 heads C1, with the qualification that he proposes two tests he holds to be reliable. (Checker: he says machines that speak in response and do tasks as well as people would not act by knowledge, which meets C1 for restricted probes.)

_Recommendation: a._ C1 concerns evidence from a test, and Descartes treats the right tests as reliable. The checker gives the same result if C1 is read to require a test.

- a: C1's headline moves from Weizenbaum (1966) to Jefferson (1949). Descartes appears on C1 as context.
- b: C1's headline moves to Descartes (1637), with his two tests in the qualification.

### 7. Čapek's _R.U.R._: are the Robots machines?

Needs your judgement: the checker and the preparer disagree.

The play also calls them machines: the Czech text of 1920 says "živé a inteligentní pracovní stroje" ("living and intelligent working machines"). The pack records them on W01 and B6, but only as context on U02 "A machine could think". The checker reads the play as stating U02 and says the boundary needs one rule for all three. No headline changes either way, because Butler 1863 is earlier.

- **a.** Count the Robots as machines throughout: _R.U.R._ states W01, B6 and U02, as fiction, with the qualification that they are synthetic organisms.
- **b.** Keep the pack's split: W01 and B6 as fiction with the qualification; U02 as context.
- **c.** Treat the Robots as outside machine intelligence: context only everywhere.

_Recommendation: a._ The play's own word for them is "machines". One boundary across the three propositions is easier to explain, and the qualification lets the reader judge the fit.

- a: _R.U.R._ appears on W01, B6 and U02, labelled fiction.
- b: _R.U.R._ appears on W01 and B6, and as context on U02.
- c: _R.U.R._ appears as context only.
