**Quick OKs: 1, 2, 4, 5, 8, 9, 11, 12, 13, 14, 17, 18; needs judgement: 3, 6, 7, 10, 15, 16.**

The pack records what central banks, the BIS, the IMF, the FSB and financial supervisors have said about machine intelligence, on twelve propositions about financial stability, monetary policy, payments and supervision, plus 30 institutional events. The decision that matters most is **3, how the "Earliest located" headline is chosen** when the earliest text is a speech or a staff paper rather than an institution's own text. After the check, the next most consequential are **15 (Waller on U07)** and **10 and 16 (which bodies count)**, because each can move a headline.

**State.** Nothing is admitted. The cross-family check has run. A model from a different maker (OpenAI's GPT, run through Codex) checked 107 items on 2026-11-03: 12 searches for the earliest text and 35 passage readings done blind, then compared; 30 further records and all 30 events checked directly; and 14 addresses Claude could not reach. "Quick: OK the recommendation" marks a mechanical, low-stakes decision whose recommendation follows from the editorial policy or from the two model families agreeing. "Needs your judgement" marks an editorial or historical judgement, or a disagreement between the checker and the preparer.

**Voice** means who a text speaks for: the institution, a committee, staff (with the usual disclaimer that views are the authors' own), or one official in a speech.

### 7. U08, AI agents and tokenised money

Needs your judgement: the checker and the preparer disagree on whether a text states it.

U08's criterion has two parts: agents' transactions would use or favour tokenised money or stablecoins; or AI would interact with such money "in ways that matter for the monetary system". The BIS Annual Economic Report 2025 (chapter III) says that, in correspondent banking on a tokenised platform, pre-screening checks and compliance reporting could be embedded in the payment instruction "through AI agents".

- **a.** Keep U08 with context records only, shown as "no located text meets the criterion"; keep MAS-convened industry papers out of scope. (Preparer: the agents do compliance reporting; the report calls them copilots for suspicious-activity reports and does not say this matters for the monetary system.)
- **b.** Count the BIS 2025 passage as stating the second part, with a qualification that it concerns compliance and states no need for stablecoins. (Checker: AI agents inside payment instructions on tokenised money, in a chapter on the next monetary system, meet the second part as worded.)
- **c.** Narrow the criterion to the first part and keep a.
- **d.** Drop U08.

_Recommendation: a._ The second part asks for an effect on the monetary system, and the passage describes compliance work. If you read the second part more broadly, b follows.

- a: U08 appears with "no located text meets the criterion" and four context texts.
- b: U08 gets a headline: the BIS, June 2025, institutional text.
- c: as a, with a narrower question.
- d: the proposition does not appear.
