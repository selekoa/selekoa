## P1. Rule B: applied

Written into `scope.md` ("What a cell is"), with a link to this record: a cell is "stated" when a passage meets the criterion as worded, however conditional or narrow its scope, with the condition or scope in the note; "stated, partly" only when the passage has what the criterion asks for in a weaker form than the criterion's own words (a domain named but excluded, or named only as possible future work; no named person or body where one is asked for; an example where a general definition is asked for; a "may" where a statement that something will happen is asked for; information sent to a board where a review role is asked for); "not found" when no passage names the thing.

Also changed: README decision 4 (now decided), and the status wording that `render.mjs` writes into `pack.md` (legend: "S~ = stated, partly: the edition has what the criterion asks for only in a weaker form than its words"; cell label: "stated, partly (a weaker form than the criterion’s words)"). `crosscheck.mjs` was not changed: it generates the historical cross-family brief of 2026-10-15, whose wording must stay as it was given to the reviewer. The app has no status wording for the pack.
