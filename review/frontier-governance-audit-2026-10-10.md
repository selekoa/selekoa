# Frontier-governance increment: machine audit, 2026-10-10

Scope: 24 claims, 54 evidence records and 4 sources added for the question `frameworks-to-obligations`: selected provisions of the EU AI Act as published in 2024 (18 claims), of the 2026 Digital Omnibus amendments (5) and one announcement record on New York's RAISE Act (1). All records are machine-checked seeds, not editorially accepted.

## Method

The proposing session retrieved the Official Journal HTML of both regulations and two New York Governor releases, extracted their text, and matched every quotation mechanically against it (whitespace normalised) before storing it. The 2024 text was also compared with the Internet Archive capture of 2024-07-12 (identical apart from the numbering of footnote markers); the 2026 text with the capture of 2026-07-28 (identical).

Two fresh-context subagents (Claude models, same family as the proposer; model version not recorded by the harness) then received only the claims, locators, quotations and the extracted source texts, not the proposing pass's reasoning. They did not edit records.

- Auditor A: all 18 EU AI Act claims and their 33 evidence items.
- Auditor B: the 5 Omnibus claims, the New York claim and a second look at 8 AI Act claims (risky: `aia-art113-general-date`, `aia-art113-chapter-v-date`, `aia-art56-adequacy-assessment`, `aia-art101-gpai-fines`, `aia-art51-compute-presumption`; seeded random draw by SHA-256 of the claim ID with a fixed label: `aia-enacted`, `aia-art55-model-evaluation`, `aia-art3-systemic-risk-definition`).

Coverage: every claim was checked by at least one auditor; 8 by both. All 45 quotation items present at the time were found word for word in the named source. Locators, including the Omnibus point numbers, were confirmed. Neither auditor fetched EUR-Lex independently; both used the supplied extracted text. Evidence items added after the audit in response to it (enacting formulas, Official Journal header lines, the Article 6(5) chapeau of the Omnibus) were matched mechanically only.

This audit is not a different-model check. The editorial policy requires one only for contested and assessment records, and this increment has none.

## Retrieval limits

- The legislature's site for New York refused automated access (HTTP 403), so no bill or session-law text was inspected.
- The Internet Archive refused lookup requests (HTTP 429) on 2026-10-10; no capture is cited for the two New York releases.
- Anthropic's Frontier Compliance Framework could not be retrieved: the trust portal that serves it loads its content by script, and the archived captures of its pages hold only the page shell. No claim was added.
- The Code of Practice, SB 53 and Internet Archive statements in qualifications were not part of the audit.
