# Kinds of AI and technical history: admission, 2026-10-31

Admission, in two parts, of the candidate pack in [`research/technical-history/`](../research/technical-history/README.md): eleven families of methods and seven names used in the history of machine intelligence, the located texts that name and define them, attributed statements about who named or devised something, and dated statements of what texts report about systems, results and releases, 1943–2023. It follows the maintainer's decisions of 2026-10-30, which adopt every recommended answer of the [pack report](technical-history-pack-2026-10-29.md), the maintainer's adjudication of 2026-10-31 of the [cross-family check](../research/technical-history/cross-family-check-2026-10-29.md), and the corrections that check lists.

- **Files copied, not merged.** The pack files, the review note and the check's two report files were copied into this branch from `technical-history-pack` (which holds the latest decisions) and `codex/technical-history-check` with `git checkout <branch> -- <paths>`. Neither branch was merged: an early commit on the pack branch quoted a historical author's address from a retyped copy header, which a later commit replaced. Every copied file was searched for email addresses after copying; none was found, so nothing was redacted. `pnpm check:privacy` passes.
- **Script:** [`scripts/technical-history-admission.ts`](../scripts/technical-history-admission.ts) builds every record from the pack and the authored [map](technical-history-admission-map-2026-10-31.json). `--part 1` then `--part 2` write the records; `--part N --check` rebuilds a part and compares it with the stored data (part 1's check holds only before part 2 is written). A part is idempotent: where its before-snapshot is applied to the stored data, the script removes the rows the snapshot lists as added, restores the rows it changed and rebuilds them; where the snapshot is absent or was made on another base, the stored data is the base and a new snapshot is written with the current commit.
- **Before-snapshots:** [`technical-history-admission-1-before-2026-10-31.json`](technical-history-admission-1-before-2026-10-31.json) and [`technical-history-admission-2-before-2026-10-31.json`](technical-history-admission-2-before-2026-10-31.json), made on branch key-papers-admission (v0.18.0), the previous admission of the 2026-10-31 chain. They go first in the chain in `src/editorial/historical-views.ts`, part 2's before part 1's.
- **Schema:** part 1 is unchanged (2.25, from the key papers admission); part 2 is 2.26 (below).
- **Editorial state:** every record is a seed. No evidence is human-reviewed, and no human review field was filled.
- **Quotations:** every quotation rests on the pack's mechanical match against its own retrieval of 2026-10-29 (`research/technical-history/verification.json`) and on the cross-family check, which re-read 87 records directly. No fresh retrieval of the pack's sources was made at admission. The one quotation the check added (Schwartz 1986) was matched again in a direct retrieval on 2026-10-31 whose SHA-256 equals the check's; the NIPS 2007 workshop page, which only the check could open, is given as a paraphrase of its reading.

## Schema 2.26 (part 2): a minor release

A new collection type with a schema change, so part 2 is a **minor** release (decision 4; [dataset-format](../docs/dataset-format.md#versions)). Part 1 changes no schema and defines no collection, so on its own it would be a patch; if both parts ship together, the release is one minor.

- **`uses_term`** (`src/domain/predicates.ts`): document → concept, a naming record.
- **`entity.naming`** (`src/domain/schema.ts`): `kind` (`family` or `term`), `criterion`, `terms`, and an optional `withheld` role with its reason and date (the rule of schema 2.16).
- **`attributes.naming`**: `string`, `sense` (`meets`, `other-sense`, `unverified`), `role` (`use`, `earliest-located`), `register`, `reading` (`text` or `indexed-title`), `claimed` and `attested` dates. The existing contract-term field `attributes.term` (schema 2.21) kept its name, so the pack's `term` block is admitted as `naming`.
- **`attributes.technical.kind`**: `definition`, `description`, `assessment`, `method` (decision 10), on `discusses` only.
- **`explicitly_credits`** may name a literal actor (decision 8: Jacqueline Roussel, named only in Kowalski's attribution, gets no person record).
- **Validation:** a `uses_term` record, and only one, carries naming attributes and names a concept with a criterion; per concept exactly one use that meets the criterion is earliest located unless the role is withheld, and then none; the holder needs dated wording, and no use meeting the criterion is dated definitely before it, by the attested date where there is one.
- **Release format 1.9** (`src/release/format.ts`, `build.ts`): the entity field `naming` and the CSV column `naming_json`; a release refuses a field its format does not list.
- Recorded in [`docs/ontology.md`](../docs/ontology.md) and [`docs/dataset-format.md`](../docs/dataset-format.md). `src/ui/mapGrammar.ts` classes `uses_term` as an intellectual relation.

## Admission's own changes

- **Statements dated by their text.** Schema 2.20 dates a stated statement by its expression. The pack dated event statements (matches, project starts, a council meeting) by the event; they are now dated by the text that states them, with the reported date in the statement and the note. arXiv copies are dated by the submission date of the version read, as the dataset's earlier arXiv sources are.
- **Dates outside the schema's bases** (submission, conference, claimed, event) keep their kind in the note and carry no basis, as the dataset's arXiv records do.
- **Reused sources** where the dataset holds the same address; **works known only from citation** take the citing text as their source (ChatGPT's announcement, Goertzel and Wang's 2007 volume).
- **Reader text.** Record IDs became "recorded separately" or were rewritten, and the script now refuses any stored person, organisation or document ID in reader text as well as the pack's; references to the pack's drafts, groups and audits were rewritten or removed.
- **Author lists.** Where the pack printed "et al.", only the named authors are recorded and the source's version says it credits further authors.
- **Methods named** on the four reported-use records (the schema requires one).
- **Subtypes** from the closed list; the SL4 message is a "Mailing-list message", as in the dataset.
- **Source roles of attributed statements.** A text's own attribution is the primary source for the record of that attribution (ontology: the role is relative to the claim), so the pack's `secondary` role is corrected to `primary` on three `explicitly_credits` records (Goertzel on Legg; Sutton and Barto on Minsky; Buchanan and Shortliffe on Feigenbaum), each with a note, as the dataset's other `explicitly_credits` records already have it.
- **Each source dated by its own expression.** A document with several versions dates each source by the version it is (Pearl's two reports); a copy that is a later revision carries its version date (GPS, 9 February 1959).
