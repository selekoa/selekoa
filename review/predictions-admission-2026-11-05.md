## Schema 2.32

- Predicate `states_prediction`: subject a document, object the predicted event as a literal; dated by the document (`time.kind: event`), never by the target.
- `attributes.prediction`: `form`, `mode`, `predictor` (`kind`, `label`, `entityIds`, `reporter`), `event` (`type`, `text`, `note`), `horizon` (`asStated`, `words`, `kind`, `years`, `interval`, `relation`, `precision`, `note`), `occasion`, `probability` and `confidence` (as worded), `points`, `conditions`, `hedge`, `survey`, `bet`, `capture`, `attested`, `retrospective`, `dispute`. No outcome field. Enumerations in `src/domain/prediction-fields.ts`.
- Validation: event, horizon, probability and confidence words in a supporting quotation (or the quoted words of a labelled paraphrase); years only for absolute and quantile horizons, printed in the horizon and in a passage; a relative horizon keeps its interval; an occasion when the horizon counts from one; a survey's figure belongs to the survey, a platform figure to the platform with a dated capture, a bet names its parties, an unnamed predictor has a reporter; `points` where a quantile horizon has several years; contested or unresolved only with a documentary dispute; attested and retrospective dates in order; no fulfilment, ranking or priority words outside quotation marks in the statement, qualification and notes.
- Collection kind `predictions` (`/predictions/<id>`), added to the release's collection kinds, as `guide` was at 0.12.0. Release format stays 1.10 (no file or column changes).
- Documented in `docs/dataset-format.md` and `docs/ontology.md`.
- **Predictor entities.** `entityIds` link only predictors the dataset already holds (Turing, Good, Vinge, Yudkowsky, Bostrom, Moravec, Legg, Karnofsky, Cotra, Aschenbrenner, Altman, Amodei, OpenAI, Anthropic, Google DeepMind; the Dartmouth signatories; Bengio; Altman, Brockman and Sutskever; Kokotajlo). No person was added (see open choice 3).

## The page

`/predictions/predictions-on-record`, listed on the Ideas hub as a tool (`ToolLink`), with a CSV at `/predictions/predictions-on-record.csv` (one row per record, the table's columns, no outcome column).

- **Chart:** source date across (1945–2030), printed target year up (1990–2160), fixed axes so filtering never rescales; one mark per printed year, a thin line joining one record's years; shapes by form (circle, square, diamond, triangle, cross), one ink colour; no present-day line, diagonal, shading or colour for past target dates; native titles on hover; no motion. A mark opens its record through the table's link, so the record loads first.
- **Table:** the same records, keyboard-operable, in a focusable scrolling region: source date with marks (forecast or aim; reported later; reported statement; wording attested from), predictor, the event in quotation marks or marked as a paraphrase with its document, horizon as stated with the year link, probability as stated, form. Order by source date or by printed target year.
- **Filters:** form (checkboxes) and decade of the source date; they narrow the chart and the table together; a status line gives what is shown.
- **Text:** introduction, what the collection does not do, the legend of forms, the marks, limits, and "How we searched" (scope, methods, gaps, read and not recorded), to `docs/style-guide.md`; the self-check patterns find nothing in the new reader text.
- **Code:** `src/ui/Predictions.tsx` and `predictions.css` load only with the page (`page-modules.tsx`); the reader text `src/reading/predictions.ts` loads with it; shared code reads `src/reading/predictions-page.ts` and `predictions-frame.ts`. Domain: `src/domain/predictions.ts`, `predictions-csv.ts`.
