# Pre-launch audit, 2 November 2026

The last quality gate before Selekoa is mentioned in public: the 20 reader
tasks of [29 October](reader-tasks-2026-10-29.md) rerun on the live site with
five new ones, the consistency audit rerun and extended, the most-read pages
checked against their sources, and a stratified random sample of 164 claims
re-checked against the documents they cite. Its job was to find the errors an
expert visitor would find in the first ten minutes. Clear, mechanical errors
are fixed on branch `prelaunch-fixes` (data release **v0.23.3** and an interface
commit); judgement calls are listed for the maintainer with a recommendation
each. Machine-readable companion: [`prelaunch-audit-2026-11-02.json`](prelaunch-audit-2026-11-02.json)
(every finding, every sampled claim with its verdict, every reader task).

Base: `main` at 4629608, dataset v0.23.2, which the live site
(selekoa.pages.dev, selekoa.org) served from the first reader run on.
Auditors: Claude-family agents throughout (readers, page auditors, sample
checkers, fixers and skeptics). Severity follows the brief: **high**, a wrong
fact on a headline or reader path; **medium**, a wrong or misleading fact
elsewhere, or a broken reader path; **low**, wording or style.

### 4. Stratified random sample

**Design.** Claims were grouped by the release that admitted them: eleven
strata for the admissions since 2026-10-28 (v0.10.1 to v0.23.2, 2,938 claims)
and one for everything earlier (2,847). The sample was weighted toward the
recent strata: 112 targeted checks against 40 for the earlier stratum, with a
minimum of 8 per stratum. Within each stratum the order was drawn with a fixed
seed (20261102); checkers worked down the list and skipped a claim only when
no faithful copy of its source could be read, so retrieval failures were
replaced by the next claim in random order. Eight independent checkers
(Claude family, fresh context, no access to each other's verdicts) re-read the
passage, locator, statement, date (value, kind, precision), qualification,
proposition role and links. Two checkers went past their targets in list
order; those 11 claims are included (they are random draws too).

**Error rate.** 1 material error in 164 checked claims: **0.6 %, 95 % Wilson
interval 0.1–3.4 %**; weighted by stratum population, 0.3 %. Since
2026-10-28: 1 in 118 (0.1–4.6 %). Before: 0 in 46 (0–7.7 %). The error
(`ht-w01-faggella-2023-worthy-successor`, PA-12) is a qualification that
contradicts its own record. Including minor slips, 36 of 164 records (22 %,
16–29 %) have something an expert could flag; most are locators one paragraph
off, metadata (a byline given as the publisher) and notes that misdescribe a
quotation's ellipsis. The verification workflow corrected the minor slips the
source settles and swept their siblings (PA-36 to PA-41).

**Limits.** A bounded sample does not certify unsampled records. The 95 %
interval is wide because the sample is small; it rules out an error rate above
about 3–5 % for records like these, not rare errors in particular places, and
the targeted page audits found more than the sample did. Seven sampled claims
could not be read (openai.com, Quartz, a WeChat captcha, Meta's framework
address returning 500, web.archive.org unreachable); replacing them biases the
sample slightly toward retrievable sources. Proposition-role judgements were
checked by one agent each. All checks are machine checks by one model family.
