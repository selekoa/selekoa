Run the parts in order.

Quotations, printed names and citations rest on the pack's mechanical check of 2026-10-28 ([`verification.json`](../research/key-papers/verification.json)) and on the cross-family check; venue versions the check located rest on its reading of 2026-10-30, and each source says so.

- **Editorial state:** every record is a seed. No evidence is human-reviewed.

## Schema

- **2.24** (part 1): predicate `states_contribution` (document → literal) and `attributes.priorityClaim`. Validation: explicit and supported; a supporting primary quotation of an expression of the document (a source naming it, or one of its own sources); dated as an event by that expression's publication or version date, or unknown when it has none; the flag only on this predicate.
- **2.25** (part 3): predicate `credits_work` (document → document) and `attributes.attribution` (`builds_on`, `attributes_term`, `summarises`, `attributes_and_disputes`). Validation: explicit and supported, an attribution kind, a supporting primary passage of the citing document; the kind only on this predicate.
- Interface: the document page lists "What the document says it adds" with the quotation and the priority flag, and the relations "Attributes ideas to" and "Ideas attributed to this work by"; the inspector shows the flag and the kind of attribution; dossiers count `credits_work` with the other documentary relations; `docs/ontology.md` and `docs/dataset-format.md` describe both versions. No subtype list changes.
- `src/reading/key-papers.ts` holds the two bases (basis-1 works; basis-2 works with the index, query, filters, rank, count and date) and `src/domain/key-papers.ts` computes the list from stored records, recounting citing documents at each release and listing by date. **Not wired into a hub:** the hub section of decision 1 would ship its list with the start-page summary, which every page loads, and the start page's projected size after the pending packs is 111.2 of 115 KB. Placement needs a build and a measurement this task did not run. No collection was created.

## Guide budget fix

The first build of this admission failed the size budget of the frameworks-and-law guide: 677.2 KB of 630. The guide computed its latest-check date from every evidence record's check date, which put every claim and passage in the guide's slice. At the maintainer's request (2026-10-31), the page now takes that date from the build's site facts, which compute the same value from the whole dataset; exports and releases still compute it from the data (commit "Draw the frameworks-and-law guide without reading every passage"). The two levers named in the [loading record of 2026-10-31](data-loading-c4-2026-10-31.md) were not needed. With the date taken from the site facts, the guide's slice no longer held every claim, but the page then failed its slice check and fell back to the whole relation core: its link to the commitments page counts signatory records by relation alone, which the slice did not hold. That count's records are now marked with `selected()`, as the commitments page's are (commit "Mark the guide's signatory count as a selection by relation"). Sizes after both fixes: the guide is 364.5 KB of 630 (608.9 KB on the previous branch, 677.2 KB before the fix), drawn from its own slice; no page needs the relation core.

## Dossier connections (maintainer decision, 2026-10-31)

Under "Why this document is included", a dossier showed one connection per document: the institution's publication, else a member's authorship of the document, else the earliest member's work that the document cites. The admission's new authorship records changed that choice silently: the Anthropic dossier's Weak-to-Strong group showed Kingma's Adam link instead of Leike's reward-modelling paper. The maintainer decided to show every qualifying connection in a stated order rather than pick one, and to apply no rule based on which admission a record came from. `institutionalDossier` (`src/domain/dossier.ts`) now lists them all: the institution's own publication first, then the document's authors in the dossier, then works by the institution or its people that the document cites, by the date of that record. Each keeps the person's record at the institution. The dossier shows the first three and lists the rest under "and N more", a disclosure that expands in place. For Weak-to-Strong in the Anthropic dossier the first three are Kingma (Adam, 2014), Dario Amodei and Jan Leike (Deep reinforcement learning from human preferences, 2017); Leike's reward-modelling link is among the rest.

The browser suite showed that the explorer map computes a layout of the whole dataset on the main thread before its first draw, at a cost that grows roughly with the square of the number of entities: about 14 s on `main`, 14.7 s with the people admission, 17.5 s with AI in biology and 20.5 s with this admission, measured in Node; in the browser the map appeared 13–14 s after the click on an idle machine.
