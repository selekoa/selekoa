## Schema 2.22

- **`states_evaluation_result`** (`src/domain/predicates.ts`): a report states the result of an evaluation it ran or commissioned, in its own words. `attributes.evaluation` carries four fields — what was measured, by what kind of method, the result in the report's words, and the caveats the report itself gives. Validation requires all four, explicit derivation and a supported resolution, a supporting primary passage of the report itself, and that the record is dated by its report; a report that states no date of its own carries an unknown date or a snapshot of the copy served. `evaluatedSystem` now belongs to this predicate as well as to `evaluation_engagement`; `activityScope` stays with the engagement.
- **Provision kinds `date` and `standing`** (decision 4): a date a text states for its own application, and the standing a text claims for itself. Three part 1 records move onto them: the 2010 guidance's stated date of effect and the framework's stated date of application become `date`, and the 2010 guidance's statement that it is voluntary becomes `standing`. Each qualification says so, and no evidence changes.
- **Decision 3 needed no change:** `evaluation_engagement` has admitted a public body as the evaluator since schema 2.20, so the UK institute's two records are admitted as structured, and the pack's open question is answered by the schema as it stands.
- Recorded in [`docs/ontology.md`](../docs/ontology.md) and [`docs/dataset-format.md`](../docs/dataset-format.md). No file, column or top-level release field changes, so the release format stays 1.8.
- The inspector shows the four fields of a stated result, with the report's caveats always beside them.

## Admission's own changes

- **Dates.** A stated result is dated by its report. Seven records of undated developer cards carry an unknown date, and one record of a transparency page a snapshot, as the schema now allows. A card that states only a last-updated date carries the revision basis.
- **Two stored sources** (the o1 system card and the ChatGPT Agent system card) are the exact expressions of documents the dataset already held, but carried no document identity; part 2 adds it, so that each stated result rests on its own report. Nothing else about those rows changes.
- **Publisher identity** is taken from the document's own publisher where that entity is in the dataset, which is what the engagement rule needs to attribute a passage to an evaluator or a developer.
- **Reader text.** Two engagement records named a dataset entity and a schema file in their qualifications; both are reworded, and the second drops a question the schema has since answered.
- **One report, one document.** The evaluations and arguments passes each retrieved the same RAND red-team report, under different keys and with identical bytes. The admission keeps one document and one source for it, and the argument record cites the document the evaluation records cite.
- **Subtypes.** System cards and technical reports take the closed list's `Technical report`; `Model card`, `Preprint`, `Report`, `Working paper`, `Blog post`, `Announcement` and `Web page` cover the rest.
