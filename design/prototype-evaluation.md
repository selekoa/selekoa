# Prototype evaluation checklist

> Direction update: the A/B/C treatment exploration has concluded. Use this checklist to evaluate successive iterations of [the Selekoa visual system — v2](visual-language-v2.md), including Light and Dark presentation preferences, rather than maintain competing canonical treatments. Historical evaluation criteria are retained below.

Use this checklist for each future candidate. **No designs are ranked here.** Record pass / partial / fail / not tested, the concrete task attempted, and an observable result. A pleasing screenshot does not demonstrate interaction quality.

## Semantic checks

- [ ] No new relationship is inferred from proximity, shared employment, chronology or a visual grouping.
- [ ] Literal round totals and spending plans remain non-edge claims; no artificial amount nodes.
- [ ] The xAI round total is not assigned to NVIDIA's participation.
- [ ] “Up to” and “more than” remain visible; overlapping tranches and cumulative amounts are not summed.
- [ ] Planned Colossus expansion remains distinct from reported operation; AWS commitment is not rendered as completed spending.
- [ ] Google DeepMind's internal compute use does not resemble an external funding transaction.
- [ ] Supersession, content change, citation and application remain separate relations.
- [ ] A historical role is not presented as a current appointment; unknown end dates stay unknown.
- [ ] Collapsing a family, filtering or changing views preserves a clear route to the underlying individual claims.
- [ ] Missing fixture evidence/review fields never become “verified”, “accepted” or an invented confidence score.

## Comparison record

For each candidate, retain its identifier/version, viewport/device, tasks attempted, checklist results, observed failures and unresolved questions. Apply the same tasks and dataset to each candidate. Treat lost direction, merged claims, hidden evidence or altered factual meaning as blocking defects; do not average them away with visual polish.
