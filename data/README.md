## Provenance cautions

- Personnel biographies support prior roles but do not establish unreported employment boundaries. Undated mutable biographies have `publicationDate: null`; access dates are separate. Unknown claim dates remain visible at every cumulative year cutoff.
- The personnel slice distinguishes individual co-founding credits, launch and departure announcements, Google's DeepMind acquisition, Brain's internal-team identity and the later Google DeepMind combination. It does not encode inferred institutional succession or ideology through shared personnel. See [scope and unresolved questions](../docs/personnel-continuity.md).

- Good's scan is mirrored under a filename containing 1966. Virginia Tech's reprint metadata corroborates the 1965 publication year; its own 2005 issued date and 2019 accession date are distinct. The catalogue is separately recorded secondary evidence.
- The learned-optimization concept claim uses arXiv version 3 dated 2021-12-01. It does not backdate inspected text to the 2019 first submission. An acknowledgement absent from the inspected version was excluded.
- The o1 card is the page labelled updated December 5, 2024 and includes multiple testing periods.
- xAI's 2024 release is on a subsequently rebranded website. This seed does not reconstruct current corporate structure.
- Amazon's amount is an announced upper bound. Sequoia's participation is separate from xAI's round total. Colossus capacity is an October 2024 supplier snapshot.

Minimal excerpts and paraphrases are attributed per source. No open redistribution licence is assumed and no bulk upstream dataset is imported.
